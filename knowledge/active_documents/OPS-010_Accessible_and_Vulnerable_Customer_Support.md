# OPS-010 — Accessible and Vulnerable Customer Support

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Audience:** All customer-facing employees
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Effective:** 01 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

A customer needs additional time, communication support or a reasonable adjustment.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-010-3.1

> Employees must ask the customer what support would help and must not make assumptions based on appearance.

### OPS-010-3.2

> Information should be explained in plain language and through an available accessible format where requested.

### OPS-010-3.3

> The customer must be addressed directly even when a companion or interpreter is present.

### OPS-010-3.4

> A safety or safeguarding concern must be escalated without unnecessarily restricting the customer's independence.

## Procedure

1. Offer help respectfully and ask the customer's preference.
2. Reduce jargon and explain one decision at a time.
3. Provide an available seating, quiet-space, large-text or communication adjustment.
4. Confirm understanding without asking for a diagnosis.
5. Escalate if an adjustment cannot be safely provided.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: routine adjustment.
- Duty manager: operational exception.
- Customer inclusion lead: recurring or complex barrier.

## Required records

- Adjustment requested only where needed
- Outcome
- Escalation reference; do not record unnecessary medical detail

## Escalation rule

Escalate immediate safeguarding or safety concerns to the duty manager and designated safeguarding contact.

## Do not

- Do not demand medical proof for routine assistance.
- Do not speak only to the companion.
- Do not record a diagnosis unless formally required.
