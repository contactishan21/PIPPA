# LOG-007 — Cold-Chain Excursion in Transit

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Cold Chain Quality Director
- **Audience:** Drivers, dispatch, warehouses and quality
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.8
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

A refrigerated vehicle, container or shipment falls outside its temperature range.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-007-5.1

> Affected product must be placed on hold immediately; delivery or sale must not continue without Quality release.

### LOG-007-5.2

> Time-temperature data, seal status, route event and product/batch details must be preserved.

### LOG-007-5.3

> The driver or dispatcher must not make a product-safety decision based on duration alone.

### LOG-007-5.4

> Quality owns disposition and customer/authority notification decisions.

### LOG-007-5.5

> After Quality disposition, cold-chain disposal or write-off up to AED 5,000 requires Transport or Warehouse Manager plus Finance Manager; AED 5,001–25,000 requires Logistics Director plus Finance Manager; AED 25,001–50,000 requires Supply Chain Director plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 requires COO plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO.

### LOG-007-5.6

> Transit custody continues until an authorised store receiver has both taken physical control of the affected units and completed the approved receiving scan or signed handover. Arrival at the receiving bay, opening vehicle doors or starting unloading does not by itself transfer custody.

### LOG-007-5.7

> An excursion discovered during unloading remains governed by LOG-007 until completed custody transfer. If custody evidence is missing, disputed or inconsistent, stop the handover and have Quality define the affected batch. The same cumulative joint-control matrix applies in LOG-007 and OPS-008: up to AED 2,000—Store Duty Manager, Transport or Warehouse Manager, and Finance Manager; AED 2,001–5,000—Regional Operations Manager, Transport or Warehouse Manager, and Finance Manager; AED 5,001–25,000—Regional Operations Manager, Logistics Director, and Finance Manager; AED 25,001–50,000—Regional Operations Manager, Supply Chain Director, and Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000—Regional Operations Director, COO, and Finance Controller; above AED 250,000—Regional Operations Director, Supply Chain Director, CFO, and CEO. Quality disposition is mandatory in every band. All named roles are cumulative; none is selected by comparing job titles.

### LOG-007-5.8

> When physical damage causes or accompanies a temperature excursion, immediately secure the full affected batch and open a joint Quality and Risk assessment under LOG-006 and LOG-007. Calculate authority from the gross combined loss before expected recovery. The cumulative approval matrix is: up to AED 5,000—Distribution Manager, Transport or Warehouse Manager, and Finance Manager; AED 5,001–25,000—Transport Procurement Director, Logistics Director, and Finance Manager; AED 25,001–50,000—Transport Procurement Director, Supply Chain Director, and Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000—CPO, COO, and Finance Controller; above AED 250,000—CPO, Supply Chain Director, CFO, and CEO. The write-off and carrier claim remain separate transactions, but both use the same gross-value band and require the joint Quality and Risk assessment.

## Procedure

1. Stop affected handover and secure the load.
2. Capture calibrated temperature, logger and physical-damage evidence.
3. Record the physical-control point and receiving scan or signed-handover status for the affected units.
4. Notify dispatch, the receiving Duty Manager and Quality.
5. Where physical damage and temperature excursion overlap, secure the full batch, open a joint Quality and Risk assessment and calculate the gross combined loss before expected recovery.
6. Move to approved recovery storage if instructed.
7. Apply LOG-007 before completed custody transfer and OPS-008 afterward; when evidence is unclear, use the explicit cumulative matrix in LOG-007-5.7 and OPS-008-5.7.
8. Record the write-off and carrier claim separately using the explicit cumulative combined-value matrix in LOG-007-5.8, then trace impacted deliveries.

## Approval and decision authority

- Quality Specialist: product-safety disposition; this does not by itself approve the financial write-off.
- Transport or Warehouse Manager plus Finance Manager: disposal/write-off up to and including AED 5,000 after Quality disposition.
- Logistics Director plus Finance Manager: AED 5,001–25,000.
- Supply Chain Director plus Finance Manager: AED 25,001–50,000.
- COO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.
- Cold Chain Director: customer or authority notification regardless of value.

## Required records

- Logger data
- Route timeline
- Batch/quantity
- Seal/vehicle condition
- Physical-damage evidence
- Gross combined loss
- Joint Quality and Risk assessment
- Disposition
- Write-off approval
- Carrier-claim approval
- Receiving scan or signed handover
- Custody owner at detection
- Joint approval where disputed

## Escalation rule

Immediate escalation for customer exposure, biologic/medicine products, widespread refrigeration failure or missing logger data.

## Do not

- Do not reset the logger.
- Do not continue delivery because the product ‘still feels cold’.
- Do not net expected carrier or insurance recovery against gross combined loss to reduce authority.
- Do not infer custody transfer from location alone or complete a receiving scan retrospectively to change the approval route.
