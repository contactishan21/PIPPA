# FIN-004 — Customer Refund and Credit-Note Financial Controls

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

- **Version:** 1.1
- **Effective:** 03 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

An approved customer refund or credit note must be processed and reconciled.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### FIN-004-4.1

> Every refund or credit note must reference the original transaction or an approved exception case.

### FIN-004-4.2

> The refund method must follow RET-001 and cannot be changed for employee convenience.

### FIN-004-4.3

> A credit note issued entirely through the authorised POS workflow is a system-issued credit note, not a manual refund. Any off-system entry, manual ledger adjustment, manual bank payment, duplicate attempt or refund above role limits requires Finance Controller approval.

### FIN-004-4.4

> Unsuccessful electronic refunds must remain traceable; a second payment method must not be used until status is confirmed.

### FIN-004-4.5

> Refund authority is: Associate up to AED 1,000 and Duty Manager for AED 1,001–5,000 under configured system controls; Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 5,001–50,000; Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000. Every off-system or manual execution requires Finance Controller approval regardless of value.

## Procedure

1. Confirm approved customer remedy.
2. Link original transaction and case.
3. Process through the authorised channel.
4. Obtain independent approval where triggered.
5. Give the customer a reference and expected bank timing.
6. Reconcile settlement and resolve failures.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: up to and including AED 1,000 for an approved standard remedy under configured system controls.
- Duty Manager: AED 1,001–5,000, including a system-issued credit note under configured system controls.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: AED 5,001–50,000.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.
- Finance Controller: every off-system/manual financial execution or duplicate-risk case regardless of value.

## Required records

- Original transaction
- Case/inspection result
- Refund reference
- Approval
- Settlement result

## Escalation rule

Escalate duplicate status, beneficiary mismatch, suspicious pattern or repeated manual refunds.

## Do not

- Do not issue two remedies for one item.
- Do not switch to cash before confirming a failed card refund.
