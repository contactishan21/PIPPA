## When to use this document

Our shipment is stuck at the border and we are being charged storage fees.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-012-4.1

> Demurrage or port storage penalties incurred during GCC cross-border transit must be logged as a supply chain defect for continuous improvement tracking.

### LOG-012-4.2

> Freight forwarders must pre-clear shipments electronically. Hard-copy original documents must be surrendered to the clearing agent a minimum of 48 hours prior to vessel or flight arrival.

### LOG-012-4.3

> Authority to absorb demurrage penalties is: Logistics Manager plus Finance Manager up to AED 10,000; Logistics Director plus Finance Director for AED 10,001–50,000.

### LOG-012-4.4

> Electronic pre-clearance is required where supported. Original hard-copy documents are supplied only where the carrier, authority or applicable law requires them and through a tracked handover.

### LOG-012-4.5

> Demurrage approval applies to the gross penalty: Logistics Manager plus Finance Manager up to AED 10,000; Logistics Director plus Finance Director for AED 10,001–50,000; VP/COO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### LOG-012-4.6

> An urgent verbal approval must include every approver required by the applicable value band and be recorded within one business day. A missed documentation deadline becomes a PRC-004 No-PO exception and remains a PRC-007 compliance breach.

### LOG-012-4.7

> Payment, supplier or forwarder recovery, and accounting write-off are separate decisions and records; a proposed chargeback does not reduce gross approval value.

## Procedure

1. Confirm that the request falls within this policy's scope and identify the transaction, employee, vendor, asset or incident involved.
2. Collect the source records and calculate the complete value, time period and related transactions before choosing an approval band.
3. Apply the controlling clauses and all referenced policies; pause the process where safety, identity, custody, legality or evidence is unresolved.
4. Obtain each required functional and financial approval before commitment, release, payment, posting or disposal.
5. Record the decision, approvers, evidence, system references and closure outcome, then complete any required reconciliation or follow-up.

## Approval and decision authority

- The operational authority stated in the clauses governs the functional decision.
- Any spend, refund, credit, loss, write-off, settlement or commitment also requires the applicable FIN-001 financial approval; the stricter cumulative combination applies.
- No approver may self-approve, split related transactions or treat investigation, validation or proof acceptance as financial approval.

Escalate immediately to Legal and the Logistics Director if a shipment is detained by customs due to suspected intellectual property violations or missing conformity certificates.
