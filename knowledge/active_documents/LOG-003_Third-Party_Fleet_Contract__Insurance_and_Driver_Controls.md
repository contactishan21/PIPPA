# LOG-003 — Third-Party Fleet Contract, Insurance and Driver Controls

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## When to use this document

A third party will carry company goods or receive customer delivery details.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-003-5.1

> A signed company-approved contract or framework call-off is mandatory before mobilisation.

### LOG-003-5.2

> The carrier must provide valid commercial, motor, cargo and employer/worker insurance at the limits specified by Risk.

### LOG-003-5.3

> Drivers must be licensed, verified, trained on conduct and delivery procedure, and identifiable to customers.

### LOG-003-5.4

> Customer data must be limited to what is required for delivery, used only for that purpose and deleted according to the contract.

### LOG-003-5.5

> Subcontracting is prohibited without prior written approval.

## Procedure

1. Use the approved contract or call-off.
2. Collect and validate insurance and licence evidence.
3. Verify drivers/vehicles and required training.
4. Configure secure delivery-data access and POD.
5. Complete readiness sign-off before first dispatch.
6. Monitor incidents, subcontracting and expiry dates.

## Approval and decision authority

- Legal: contract deviations.
- Risk/HSE: insurance and safety.
- Privacy: customer-data controls.
- Transport director: operational readiness.

## Required records

- Signed contract
- Insurance
- Driver/vehicle register
- Training
- Privacy instruction
- Readiness approval

## Escalation rule

Stop mobilisation for expired insurance, unapproved subcontractors, unverifiable drivers or insecure data sharing.

## Do not

- Do not send addresses through personal messaging apps.
- Do not accept ‘insurance pending’.
