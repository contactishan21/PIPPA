# MKT-003 — Co-Op Marketing Funds and Vendor Rebates

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

- **Status:** CURRENT

- **Effective:** 04 September 2026

## When to use this document

The vendor agreed to pay for part of this campaign.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### MKT-003-4.1

> Brand-funded marketing campaigns (Co-Op) must have a signed vendor agreement dictating the exact percentage or fixed value to be claimed back based on sell-through performance.

### MKT-003-4.2

> Marketing must supply Finance with verified proof of performance (e.g., live campaign photos, social media metrics) before the vendor rebate invoice is issued.

### MKT-003-4.3

> Head of Marketing approves proof of performance for campaigns up to AED 50,000; Commercial Director approves campaigns exceeding AED 50,000.

### MKT-003-4.4

> Campaign commitment approval, proof-of-performance acceptance and rebate-claim approval are separate controls and must be performed by authorised roles with documented segregation.

### MKT-003-4.5

> The signed agreement must state eligible spend, calculation basis, evidence, claim deadline, tax treatment, currency and dispute route before campaign launch.

### MKT-003-4.6

> The total campaign commitment follows FIN-001. Proof acceptance by Head of Marketing up to AED 50,000 or Commercial Director above AED 50,000 does not approve spend or write-off a rejected rebate.
