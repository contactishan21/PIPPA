# FIN-005 — Petty Cash and Operational Cash Advance

## When to use this document

A team needs small urgent cash expenditure or an operational advance.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### FIN-005-4.1

> Petty cash is company-controlled cash issued before or at the time of an urgent low-value business purchase when an approved purchasing method is impractical. An employee expense claim reimburses an employee after the employee used personal funds for a necessary, authorised and unavoidable business cost.

### FIN-005-4.2

> A single petty-cash transaction may not exceed AED 500 and related purchases must not be split.

### FIN-005-4.3

> Cash advances require a named custodian, purpose, amount and retirement date.

### FIN-005-4.4

> Unused cash and receipts must be returned within five business days after the activity.

### FIN-005-4.5

> Petty cash is limited to AED 500. Operational cash advances from AED 501–50,000 require Director plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 require VP/COO plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 require CFO plus CEO.

### FIN-005-4.6

> An operational cash advance must not normally be used for AED 500 or less; an approved petty-cash facility should be used. If no petty-cash facility is available and personal payment is unreasonable, the requesting employee's Department Manager plus Finance Manager may approve a micro-advance up to AED 500 using the same named-custodian, receipt, unused-cash return and five-business-day retirement controls as other advances.

### FIN-005-4.7

> A missing receipt for petty cash, a micro-advance or another company-funded cash advance is a cash-accountability exception and must not be converted into an employee expense claim under FIN-002. The custodian must return unused cash, provide a written explanation and submit available independent evidence. The original advance approvers plus an independent Finance reviewer must approve the reconciliation. Any unsupported balance may be written off only after investigation and the applicable FIN-001 write-off approval; repeated or suspected loss must be escalated to Loss Prevention or Internal Audit. Repayment by the employee is not automatic and requires a documented finding and lawful recovery process.

### FIN-005-4.8

> Where alternative evidence does not support all company cash issued under FIN-005, the unexplained balance follows this cash-loss matrix: up to AED 200 requires the custodian's Department Manager plus an independent Finance checker; AED 201–10,000 requires the Department Manager plus Finance Manager and mandatory Loss Prevention review; AED 10,001–50,000 requires the Function Director plus Finance Manager and Loss Prevention Director review; AED 50,001–250,000 requires VP or COO plus Finance Controller and Loss Prevention Director review; above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO plus Internal Audit Director. Repeated loss or suspected theft, tampering or collusion requires Loss Prevention regardless of value. These controls are additional to the investigation, original advance reconciliation and any stricter FIN-001 requirement.

### FIN-005-4.9

> Found cash placed into a petty-cash container remains found property and does not become company petty cash. The custodian must stop using the container, preserve the ledger and receipts, and follow OPS-009-4.9. Any petty-cash shortage or unsupported balance identified during reconstruction remains a separate FIN-005 cash-accountability exception under FIN-005-4.7 and FIN-005-4.8; an excess is held in the found-cash suspense process and must not replenish the imprest.

### FIN-005-4.10

> An employee must not add, lend or transfer personal funds to balance petty cash, retire an advance or replace unsupported company cash. If personal funds are offered or were already added, the custodian must record the gross shortage before the payment, remove the personal amount from usable company cash under dual control and place it in a separately referenced suspense record pending Finance and HR or Legal direction. The personal payment does not reduce the investigation value, approval band or Loss Prevention trigger and cannot be reimbursed under FIN-002. Any recovery from an employee may occur only after a documented finding and lawful recovery decision under FIN-005-4.7.

### FIN-005-4.11

> Petty cash and micro-advances are limited to approved urgent operational supplies and may not exceed AED 500 per single disbursement; related purchases must not be split.

### FIN-005-4.12

> An itemised tax invoice showing the supplier's tax-registration details is required where legally applicable and reasonably obtainable. If it is missing, the transaction remains a FIN-005 exception under clauses 4.7 and 4.8; it must not be converted to FIN-002 or concealed through employee repayment.

### FIN-005-4.13

> A custodian or employee must not replace an unsupported balance with personal funds and then claim those funds through FIN-002. Any attempted substitution is recorded as part of the cash-loss investigation and Loss Prevention review.

## Procedure

1. Before the purchase, use petty cash when company-controlled cash is available and appropriate; obtain independent approval and issue it to a named custodian.
2. If the amount is AED 500 or less, petty cash is unavailable and personal payment is unreasonable, obtain the requesting employee's Department Manager plus Finance Manager approval for a micro-advance before spending.
3. Use FIN-002 only when an employee has already used personal funds for a necessary, authorised and unavoidable cost, or when prior expense authorisation specifically permitted personal payment.
4. Do not offer a choice among petty cash, micro-advance and reimbursement after the event merely for convenience.
5. Record the gross shortage before any personal payment. If personal money was added, remove it from usable company cash under dual control into a separate suspense record and refer the matter to Finance and HR or Legal under FIN-005-4.10.
6. If found cash was placed into petty cash, stop use of the container and follow OPS-009-4.9; do not use the excess to replenish the imprest or offset a shortage.
7. Collect itemised receipts, return unused cash and reconcile differences within five business days.
8. If an unsupported balance remains, complete the investigation and apply the loss approvals and Loss Prevention triggers in FIN-005-4.8 before any write-off.
9. For employee-funded costs, submit reimbursement through FIN-002 within its deadline.

## Approval and decision authority

- Manager plus independent custodian check: petty cash up to and including AED 500.
- Requesting employee's Department Manager plus Finance Manager: micro-advance up to and including AED 500 only where no petty-cash facility is available and personal payment is unreasonable.
- Director plus Finance Manager: operational advance from AED 501–50,000.
- VP/COO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.
- Unsupported cash loss up to AED 200: Department Manager plus independent Finance checker.
- Unsupported cash loss above AED 200: the value-band approvers and mandatory Loss Prevention or Internal Audit review defined in FIN-005-4.8.

## Escalation rule

Escalate missing receipts, overdue advance, cash loss, prohibited spend, repeated micro-advance requests or use of any cash channel to avoid procurement.

## Do not

- Do not use petty cash or a micro-advance for payroll, vendor deposits, gifts or personal loans.
- Do not split purchases.
- Do not select a micro-advance merely to avoid petty-cash controls.
- Do not approve the cash channel retrospectively for convenience.
- Do not allow an employee to replace, lend or top up company cash, or reimburse that payment through FIN-002.
- Do not treat found cash as petty-cash income, replenishment or a variance offset.
