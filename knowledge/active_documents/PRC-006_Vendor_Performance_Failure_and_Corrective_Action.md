# PRC-006 — Vendor Performance Failure and Corrective Action

## Control information

- **Owner:** Vendor Performance Director
- **Audience:** Contract owners, vendor managers and procurement
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.10
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

A vendor repeatedly misses service levels, supplies poor quality or creates operational risk.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### PRC-006-4.1

> Performance concerns must be supported by objective contract, SLA, quality or delivery evidence.

### PRC-006-4.2

> A material breach requires a written corrective-action plan with owner and due date.

### PRC-006-4.3

> Service credits, payment holds or termination may be applied only under contract terms and authorised review.

### PRC-006-4.4

> Critical continuity risk must be escalated before standard review timelines.

### PRC-006-4.5

> A vendor service credit, claim or settlement up to AED 50,000 requires Procurement Director plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 requires CPO plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO. Legal review is also required for waiver of material rights or non-standard settlement terms.

### PRC-006-4.6

> A breach is material when the contract defines it as material or when it creates a safety, regulatory, privacy, fraud, product-quality or critical-continuity impact; causes an SLA failure carrying a contractual remedy; or produces actual or reasonably expected exposure of AED 10,000 or more. Critical continuity risk means a failure expected to stop a critical service, prevent fulfilment of one business day or more of demand, or trigger an emergency response under PRC-007. The highest applicable trigger governs.

### PRC-006-4.7

> A supplier's refusal, delay or shortfall in issuing an agreed RTV credit is a vendor claim and does not disappear when inventory is written off. Procurement must preserve the return authorisation, outbound custody evidence, supplier receipt and original invoice, then pursue the claim or settlement under PRC-006-4.5. Legal review is mandatory where the supplier disputes title, defect responsibility, quantity received or contractual entitlement. Finance must not net an expected or disputed credit against another invoice.

## Procedure

1. Document the requirement and actual result.
2. Classify the breach and continuity impact using PRC-006-4.6 and record the highest applicable trigger.
3. Check the contract remedy and prior incidents.
4. Meet the vendor and issue a corrective-action request.
5. Track actions and verify closure.
6. Escalate commercial remedies, emergency continuity action or alternate sourcing if performance does not recover.
7. Where an approved RTV credit is refused, delayed or short, preserve the RMA, custody, invoice and supplier correspondence and pursue the vendor claim under PRC-006-4.7 without informal payment netting.

## Approval and decision authority

- Contract Owner: operational corrective plan with no financial settlement.
- Procurement Director plus Finance Manager: service credit, claim or settlement up to and including AED 50,000.
- CPO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000 or material sourcing action.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.
- Legal plus CPO: suspension, termination, waiver of material rights or non-standard settlement terms.

## Required records

- SLA evidence
- Incident log
- Vendor response
- Corrective plan
- Closure evidence
- RTV/RMA and supplier acceptance
- Credit due date and shortfall
- Linked FIN-003 reconciliation
- Supplier dispute and Legal referral where applicable

## Escalation rule

Escalate safety, fraud, data breach, insolvency or critical supply interruption immediately.

## Do not

- Do not threaten termination outside authority.
- Do not withhold undisputed payment without contractual basis.
- Do not close an RTV claim because goods were dispatched, or net a disputed supplier credit informally against another payment.
