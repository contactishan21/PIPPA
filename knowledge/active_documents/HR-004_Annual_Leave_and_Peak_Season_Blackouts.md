## Authoritative policy clauses

### HR-004-4.1

> Annual leave during a formally declared retail peak period requires Regional Operations Director exception approval. Only the exact dates and locations published in the approved leave calendar constitute a blackout period.

### HR-004-4.2

> Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 accrued leave days into the next calendar year.

### HR-004-4.3

> Blackout periods apply only when HR and Operations publish the exact dates, affected locations and exception owner in the approved leave calendar before the period begins; examples are not binding dates.

### HR-004-4.4

> A leave request must record the decision and reason within seven calendar days. A pending request is not approval and escalates to HR after that period.

### HR-004-4.5

> Carry-over above five days is permitted only where required by law or by a documented HR exception; carried days follow the published use-by date.
