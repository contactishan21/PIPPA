# OPS-003 — Store Opening and Closing Controls

## When to use this document

A store is being opened or closed, including late opening, early closure or key-holder absence.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-003-4.1

> Opening and closing must be completed by an authorised key holder with a second employee present for cash and alarm controls.

### OPS-003-4.2

> Fire exits, alarm status, refrigeration alerts and visible safety hazards must be checked before customers enter.

### OPS-003-4.3

> An early closure or delayed opening requires Regional Operations Manager approval except where immediate safety demands evacuation. Under OPS-003-4.5, the Duty Manager normally leads, but any employee may begin evacuation without prior approval when the Duty Manager is absent, incapacitated, unreachable or delay would increase credible immediate danger.

### OPS-003-4.4

> The checklist must be completed contemporaneously, not reconstructed later.

### OPS-003-4.5

> The Duty Manager normally leads a store evacuation, but life-safety authority is not exclusive to that role. Any employee who observes a credible immediate danger may activate the alarm, direct customers and colleagues to the nearest safe exit and call emergency services without waiting for approval when the Duty Manager is absent, incapacitated, unreachable or delay would increase risk. The employee must not investigate or move the hazard and must notify the Duty Manager, Security and Regional Operations as soon as people are moving to safety.

## Procedure

1. Confirm authorised staffing.
2. For credible immediate danger, activate the alarm, direct people to the nearest safe exit and call emergency services. Do not delay for a Duty Manager or regional approval when the fallback in OPS-003-4.5 applies.
3. Complete external and internal safety walk.
4. Disarm/arm security according to role segregation.
5. Count and transfer cash under dual control.
6. Complete checklist and report exceptions before handover.

## Approval and decision authority

- Duty Manager: routine open/close and immediate life-safety evacuation.
- Regional Operations Manager: planned delayed opening or early closure and post-event operational decision.
- Emergency services/security: immediate threat.
- Any employee: immediate life-safety evacuation when the Duty Manager is unavailable or delay would increase risk; Regional Operations is notified after people are moving to safety.

## Required records

- Opening/closing checklist
- Key-holder names
- Alarm exceptions
- Cash handover
- Regional approval if applicable
- Evacuation initiator and observed trigger
- Duty Manager availability
- Emergency-service/Security contact and handover

## Escalation rule

Do not enter alone if forced entry, suspicious persons, smoke, water or alarm anomalies are observed.

## Do not

- Do not share alarm codes.
- Do not backdate the checklist.
- Do not leave one employee alone with exposed cash.
