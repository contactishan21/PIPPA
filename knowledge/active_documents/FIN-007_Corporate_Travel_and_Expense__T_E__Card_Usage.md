## Authoritative policy clauses

### FIN-007-4.1

> Corporate T&E cards are restricted to authorized business expenses only; cash withdrawals, personal items, and alcohol purchases are strictly prohibited.

### FIN-007-4.2

> Misuse of the T&E card will result in immediate card suspension and documented recovery review for unapproved amounts (cross-referenced with PAY-001).

### FIN-007-4.3

> T&E Card issuance authority: Department Director plus Finance Manager for standard limits (AED 10,000/month); VP/COO plus CFO for executive limits (AED 50,000/month).

### FIN-007-4.4

> Card issuance, monthly limit and any temporary increase require a documented business need. Limits below or between the stated standard and executive bands are approved by the next applicable authority; no unassigned gap exists.

### FIN-007-4.5

> Suspected misuse triggers card suspension and investigation. Recovery from an employee is not automatic and may occur only after HR/Legal review and any consent or process required by law under PAY-001.

### FIN-007-4.6

> A genuine emergency exception requires prior Finance Director approval where practicable, itemised evidence and retrospective review; prohibited personal or cash use cannot be regularised as an emergency.
