# OPS-005 — Gift Cards, Store Credit and Credit Notes

## When to use this document

A customer wants to buy, use, replace or refund a gift card or credit note.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-005-4.1

> Gift cards and credit notes are treated as value instruments and must be verified before use or replacement.

### OPS-005-4.2

> Unused gift cards are not redeemable for cash except where required by applicable law or for a verified company error. Every cash error-correction must follow the operational and independent Finance bands in OPS-005-4.5, which align to FIN-001.

### OPS-005-4.3

> A lost instrument may be replaced only when ownership and remaining balance can be verified and the original is blocked.

### OPS-005-4.4

> Employees must not reveal a balance until customer verification is complete.

### OPS-005-4.5

> Verified gift-card or credit-note cash error-corrections follow FIN-001's role progression: Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager up to AED 10,000; Regional Operations Director plus Finance Manager for AED 10,001–50,000; VP/COO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000. The operational and Finance approvals are both mandatory, and neither approver may be the beneficiary.

## Procedure

1. Scan or enter the instrument through the approved system.
2. Verify ownership for replacement or cash error-correction requests.
3. Block the old instrument before issuing a replacement.
4. For a verified cash error, calculate the full value and obtain the operational and Finance approvals in OPS-005-4.5 before releasing cash.
5. Explain validity and use conditions.
6. Provide a transaction receipt and record the case.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: standard redemption under configured system controls.
- Duty Manager: verified replacement up to and including AED 1,000.
- Finance Controller: replacement above AED 1,000; this replacement control does not define cash error-correction authority.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: cash error-correction up to and including AED 10,000.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Manager: cash error-correction from AED 10,001–50,000.
- VP/COO plus Finance Controller: cash error-correction from AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: cash error-correction above AED 250,000.

## Required records

- Instrument reference
- Verification evidence
- Old/new balance or cash-correction value
- Operational approval
- Independent Finance approval
- Receipt

## Escalation rule

Escalate suspected cloning, bulk balance enquiries or system discrepancies.

## Do not

- Do not exchange value instruments outside the POS.
- Do not read balances aloud before verification.
