# LOG-001 — Peak-Demand External Fleet Activation

## When to use this document

Home-delivery operations are at full capacity during a peak sale and need an external fleet.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-001-5.1

> External fleet capacity may be activated only after internal fleet utilisation and demand shortfall are documented.

### LOG-001-5.2

> Approved transport vendors must be approached in ranked order unless capacity, geography or response time makes that impractical.

### LOG-001-5.3

> The approval request must state the full expected cost, delivery volume, service window and customer/data/safety controls.

### LOG-001-5.4

> A written contract or approved call-off, including an approved emergency one-page call-off, must be in place before drivers receive goods or customer information. Recorded verbal spend authority under PRC-007 may permit the commitment when systems are unavailable, but it never replaces this mobilisation control.

### LOG-001-5.5

> The initial emergency activation is limited to seven calendar days. Continuation is permitted only through the single controlled continuity extension in LOG-001-5.7 or through an approved normal sourcing and contract route; consecutive emergency-period resets are prohibited.

### LOG-001-5.6

> The integrated external-fleet matrix includes the transport-spend control required by LOG-002 and the additional fleet-activation control: Logistics Manager plus Procurement Manager plus Finance Manager up to AED 10,000; Logistics Director plus Procurement Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 10,001–50,000; COO plus CPO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000. Apply this matrix once; do not duplicate or omit roles by layering the two documents. Mandatory Legal, Privacy and HSE reviews remain additional.

### LOG-001-5.7

> The initial emergency fleet activation may last no more than seven calendar days. If the same backlog or substantially related demand continues, it is one continuing commitment and must not be restarted as a new emergency period. Before day 8, the team must aggregate actual cost, committed cost and the best estimate of all foreseeable continuation, then obtain the LOG-001-5.6 authority for that total. One continuity extension of up to seven additional calendar days may be approved by the applicable value-band approvers plus CPO and Legal only where a documented normal sourcing action is already active and stopping service creates critical-continuity risk. From day 15, capacity must operate under a written normal contract or call-off awarded through the standard sourcing route or a documented PRC-008 sourcing exception, with approval based on the total continuing commitment; further consecutive emergency activations are prohibited.

## Procedure

1. Quantify daily order backlog, internal fleet utilisation and service risk.
2. Check the approved carrier list and obtain capacity/rate confirmations.
3. Calculate total expected cost for the full activation window.
4. Apply the single integrated approval matrix in LOG-001-5.6, which incorporates LOG-002 transport-spend control, then create the PO.
5. Confirm signed call-off/contract, insurance, driver checks, data instructions and proof-of-delivery method.
6. Brief operations and monitor daily SLA, incidents and spend.
7. By day 6, either complete normal sourcing for day 8 onward or submit one pre-expiry continuity-extension request under LOG-001-5.7 using the aggregate actual, committed and foreseeable cost.
8. Within five business days after peak, reconcile invoices and complete a performance review.

## Approval and decision authority

- Logistics Manager plus Procurement Manager plus Finance Manager: up to and including AED 10,000.
- Logistics Director plus Procurement Manager plus Finance Manager: AED 10,001–50,000.
- COO plus CPO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.
- Legal, Privacy and HSE: mandatory risk reviews before customer data or goods transfer.
- One day-8 to day-14 continuity extension: the applicable LOG-001-5.6 value-band approvers plus CPO and Legal, approved before the initial activation expires.

## Required records

- Capacity calculation
- Vendor/rate evidence
- Approval
- PO and contract
- Insurance and driver controls
- Daily SLA/spend
- Close-out review
- Aggregate initial and continuation cost
- Normal sourcing action and milestones
- Pre-expiry extension approval

## Escalation rule

Escalate if no approved vendor is available, a carrier proposes subcontracting, insurance is insufficient, or customer data cannot be securely shared.

## Do not

- Do not hand goods or customer addresses to an unsigned vendor.
- Do not approve only the daily rate while ignoring the total expected cost.
- Do not restart or chain seven-day emergency activations; use the single controlled extension and normal sourcing route in LOG-001-5.7.
