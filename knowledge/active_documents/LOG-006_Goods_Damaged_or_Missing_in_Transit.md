# LOG-006 — Goods Damaged or Missing in Transit

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Distribution Quality Manager
- **Audience:** Drivers, warehouse teams, stores and customer service
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.10
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

Goods are damaged, stolen or cannot be located during loading, transit, delivery or customer handover.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-006-4.1

> Damaged goods must be isolated and must not be represented as saleable.

### LOG-006-4.2

> Custody point, packaging condition, item/batch/serial and photographs must be recorded.

### LOG-006-4.3

> Customer resolution must be coordinated separately from the carrier-liability investigation.

### LOG-006-4.4

> Unsafe, leaking, swollen-battery or contaminated items require the hazardous-goods response.

### LOG-006-4.5

> Carrier claims up to AED 5,000 require Distribution Manager plus Finance Manager; AED 5,001–50,000 require Transport Procurement Director plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 require CPO plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 require CFO plus CEO.

### LOG-006-4.6

> When physical damage causes or accompanies a temperature excursion, immediately secure the full affected batch and open a joint Quality and Risk assessment under LOG-006 and LOG-007. Calculate authority from the gross combined loss before expected recovery. The cumulative approval matrix is: up to AED 5,000—Distribution Manager, Transport or Warehouse Manager, and Finance Manager; AED 5,001–25,000—Transport Procurement Director, Logistics Director, and Finance Manager; AED 25,001–50,000—Transport Procurement Director, Supply Chain Director, and Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000—CPO, COO, and Finance Controller; above AED 250,000—CPO, Supply Chain Director, CFO, and CEO. The write-off and carrier claim remain separate transactions, but both use the same gross-value band and require the joint Quality and Risk assessment.

### LOG-006-4.7

> Goods are missing in transit when a required custody scan, sealed-load item or route inventory cannot be located before customer handover. The dispatcher must open an incident immediately, freeze the affected custody record, reconcile loading, route, scan, seal, GPS and handover evidence, and notify the carrier. The item becomes provisionally missing if it is not located within four operational hours or by route closure, whichever occurs first. The Distribution Manager must make the final located-or-lost determination by the end of the next business day; Loss Prevention may approve one documented extension of up to two business days where an active recovery lead exists. Confirmed theft, tampering, diversion or any missing event of AED 5,000 or more requires Loss Prevention review and Security or police notification where appropriate.

### LOG-006-4.8

> A missing-in-transit inventory write-off is based on the gross value of all items in the same custody event: up to AED 10,000 requires Distribution Manager plus Finance Manager; AED 10,001–50,000 requires Logistics Director plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 requires COO plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO. Any carrier claim is recorded separately and follows LOG-006-4.5 using the same gross event value; expected recovery must not reduce the write-off band. A customer non-receipt case may proceed under LOG-008, but it does not transfer or replace the inventory-loss investigation or approvals in LOG-006.

### LOG-006-4.9

> Return to Vendor applies when Quality and Procurement determine that goods are damaged, defective, non-conforming or recalled because of supplier responsibility rather than carrier handling. Until responsibility is determined, goods remain quarantined and the cause must be assessed jointly by Quality, Procurement and Transport; the case must not be classified as carrier damage or supplier fault merely to obtain a lower approval route. An RTV must reference the original PO and invoice, Quality disposition, supplier return-material authorisation or other written acceptance, item and batch or serial details, quantity, gross value and expected credit.

### LOG-006-4.10

> RTV authority is based on the gross value of all related goods in the same defect, batch, recall or return event: up to AED 10,000 requires Distribution Manager, Procurement Manager and Finance Manager; AED 10,001–50,000 requires Logistics Director, Procurement Director and Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 requires COO, CPO and Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO and CEO. Quality approval is mandatory regardless of value. Goods may leave company custody only after the approvals, written supplier authorisation, outbound inventory transaction, approved carrier booking, packing and hazardous-goods controls, and a signed or scanned custody handover. Accounts Payable must reconcile the supplier credit under FIN-003-5.7. Dispatch creates an RTV inventory-in-transit or supplier-receivable record; it does not itself create an inventory write-off or settle the vendor claim. A refused, delayed, partial or disputed credit remains open under PRC-006-4.7 until recovered or settled under that clause's authority, and any accounting write-off of the resulting receivable separately follows FIN-001.

## Procedure

1. Determine whether the event is damage, missing inventory or both; make unsafe goods safe and isolate them.
2. Freeze the custody record and preserve photographs, scans, seal, loading, route, GPS and handover evidence relevant to the event.
3. For missing goods, open the incident immediately, notify the carrier and complete the four-operational-hour or route-closure search in LOG-006-4.7.
4. For damage with a temperature excursion, secure the full batch and apply the joint assessment and matrix in LOG-006-4.6.
5. Record the final located-or-lost determination by the next business day unless Loss Prevention approves the one permitted evidence-based extension.
6. Calculate gross event value before expected recovery and obtain the inventory write-off approvals in LOG-006-4.8 and separate carrier-claim approvals in LOG-006-4.5.
7. Link any customer non-receipt case under LOG-008 to the same incident and process the customer remedy through its controlling policy.
8. Where the supplier rather than the carrier is responsible, keep the goods quarantined, complete the joint Quality, Procurement and Transport cause assessment, obtain the supplier's written RMA or acceptance, and obtain the RTV approvals in LOG-006-4.10.
9. Post the approved outbound inventory movement, book an approved carrier, preserve packing and any hazardous-goods controls, and obtain a signed or scanned custody handover before dispatch.
10. Reconcile the supplier credit under FIN-003-5.7. If the supplier refuses, delays or shorts the credit, keep the vendor claim open under PRC-006-4.7; do not create an immediate or unapproved write-off and do not use informal invoice netting.

## Approval and decision authority

- Distribution Manager plus Finance Manager: carrier claim up to and including AED 5,000.
- Transport Procurement Director plus Finance Manager: AED 5,001–50,000.
- CPO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.
- Quality Manager: safety or contamination disposition regardless of value.
- Distribution Manager plus Finance Manager: missing-in-transit write-off up to and including AED 10,000.
- Logistics Director plus Finance Manager: missing-in-transit write-off from AED 10,001–50,000.
- COO plus Finance Controller: missing-in-transit write-off from AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: missing-in-transit write-off above AED 250,000.
- Loss Prevention: suspected theft, tampering or diversion, every missing event of AED 5,000 or more, and any extension of the final determination deadline.
- RTV up to AED 10,000: Distribution Manager, Procurement Manager and Finance Manager, with Quality assessment.
- RTV AED 10,001–50,000: Logistics Director, Procurement Director and Finance Manager, with Quality assessment.
- RTV AED 50,001–250,000: COO, CPO and Finance Controller, with Quality assessment.
- RTV above AED 250,000: CFO and CEO, with Quality assessment.

## Required records

- SKU/serial
- Custody scan
- Photos
- Temperature/logger evidence where applicable
- Gross combined loss
- Joint Quality and Risk assessment
- Customer remedy
- Write-off approval
- Carrier claim
- Missing-item incident timestamp
- Seal, loading, route and GPS evidence
- Search result and deadline
- Final located-or-lost determination
- Linked LOG-008 incident reference
- Joint supplier-versus-carrier cause assessment
- Supplier RMA or written acceptance
- RTV gross value and authority record
- Outbound inventory transaction and carrier booking
- Packing controls and custody handover
- Supplier credit-note reconciliation or linked vendor claim

## Escalation rule

Escalate injury, fire risk, leakage, contamination or repeated lane/vendor patterns.

## Do not

- Do not conceal damage with repacking.
- Do not net expected carrier or insurance recovery against the gross loss to reduce authority.
- Do not ask the customer to pursue the carrier directly.
- Do not ship goods to a supplier without written return acceptance, the required approvals, an outbound inventory transaction and custody evidence.
- Do not choose the carrier-claim, RTV or write-off route solely to obtain a lower approval band, and do not net an expected supplier credit against an invoice informally.
