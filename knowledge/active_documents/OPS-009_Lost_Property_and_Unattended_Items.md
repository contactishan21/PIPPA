# OPS-009 — Lost Property and Unattended Items

## Control information

- **Owner:** Security & Loss Prevention Manager
- **Audience:** All store employees and security
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.10
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

An item is found, claimed, unclaimed or considered suspicious.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-009-4.1

> Ordinary found property must be logged, sealed where practical and stored in the designated secure location.

### OPS-009-4.2

> A claimant must describe the item and a non-public identifying feature before release.

### OPS-009-4.3

> Cash, identification, bank cards, medicines and high-value items require duty-manager custody.

### OPS-009-4.4

> A suspicious unattended item must not be touched. Employees must move people away, contact Security or emergency services, and apply OPS-009-4.11. Any employee may initiate the immediate evacuation provisions in OPS-003-4.5 and OPS-007-3.5 when danger is credible; Duty Manager absence must not cause delay.

### OPS-009-4.5

> Ordinary unclaimed property is retained for 90 calendar days; high-value non-cash property defined in OPS-009-4.10 is retained for 180 calendar days. Identification, bank cards and medicines are transferred to the issuer, police or approved authority within five business days where practicable.

### OPS-009-4.6

> After the retention period, unclaimed property is donated, recycled, securely destroyed or transferred to the appropriate authority under two-person control. Data-bearing devices must be securely destroyed or transferred without accessing their contents; unclaimed cash is paid to the designated suspense account.

### OPS-009-4.7

> Found cash is a separate retention category and is held for 90 calendar days from the recorded find date regardless of amount. It must remain in a sealed, referenced cash bag under Duty Manager custody and dual-control count. If unclaimed at expiry, the Finance Manager transfers the full amount to the designated suspense account under the controlled physical-deposit and ledger-reconciliation process in OPS-009-4.8. A police, court or regulatory direction requiring earlier transfer overrides the 90-day period and must be recorded.

### OPS-009-4.8

> At retention expiry, Finance must issue a pre-numbered found-cash transfer reference before the bag is opened. The Duty Manager and a second authorised employee must verify the seal, recount the cash and record a dedicated POS found-cash suspense till drop where that function exists. If it does not, they must prepare a tamper-evident deposit to the Finance-designated company bank account using the transfer reference and approved cash-collection route. The cash must never be recorded as a sale, refund, void, petty-cash replenishment or till-variance offset. Physical custody ends only when the approved carrier or bank acknowledges receipt, or the resealed bag returns to the safe pending collection. Finance then posts and reconciles the suspense-account entry to the POS or bank receipt.

### OPS-009-4.9

> If found cash is accidentally mixed with a till float, petty-cash tin or other company cash, the employee must notify the Duty Manager immediately and stop further use of that cash container. The Duty Manager and a second authorised employee must seal and count the entire container, preserve the last verified balance, ledger, receipts and available surveillance, and open a Found-Cash Commingling Incident. Where contemporaneous evidence establishes the found amount, that amount is segregated into a referenced bag and retains its original find date. Where it does not, Finance Manager and Loss Prevention must approve the reconstructed amount; any unresolved shortage follows FIN-005-4.7 and FIN-005-4.8, while any excess remains sealed in found-cash suspense. No journal entry, repayment or transfer closes the incident without reconciliation and dual sign-off.

### OPS-009-4.10

> For OPS-009, high-value non-cash property means any phone, laptop, tablet, jewellery or watch, or any other item with an evidenced replacement value of AED 5,000 or more. The Duty Manager records value using an item receipt, company catalogue price or two current market references in that order of preference. If reliable evidence cannot be obtained or the reasonable range crosses AED 5,000, the item is treated as high value and retained for 180 calendar days.

### OPS-009-4.11

> Credible immediate danger exists when emergency services, Security or an alarm directs evacuation, or when an unattended item is associated with an explicit threat, smoke or fire, leakage, a strong unusual odour, abnormal heat or sound, or exposed hazardous material. Any employee may activate the alarm, direct people by the nearest safe route away from the item and call emergency services from a safe location without waiting for the Duty Manager. Employees must not touch, move, open or investigate the item. Assembly-point accountability, emergency-service handover and later notification to the Duty Manager and Regional Operations are required once people are safe.

## Procedure

1. Assess from a safe distance.
2. If the item meets a credible-danger trigger in OPS-009-4.11, do not touch or investigate it; move people away, initiate evacuation if needed and call emergency services from a safe location without waiting for the Duty Manager.
3. For ordinary property, log time/location and secure it.
4. If found cash was commingled, stop using the affected cash container, notify the Duty Manager, seal and count the whole container under dual control, and follow OPS-009-4.9 before normal custody processing continues.
5. For found cash, complete a dual-control count, seal it in a referenced cash bag and calculate the 90-calendar-day expiry.
6. For claims, verify a non-public detail and record release.
7. For sensitive items, transfer to the Duty Manager and apply the special five-business-day transfer rule where relevant.
8. For suspicious items, do not touch: move people away, contact security or emergency services and evacuate immediately if danger is credible.
9. At found-cash expiry, obtain Finance's pre-numbered transfer reference before opening the bag; verify the seal and recount under dual control.
10. Use the dedicated POS suspense till drop where available, otherwise the approved tamper-evident bank-deposit and cash-collection route; retain the receipt and reconcile it to the Finance ledger entry.
11. Review ordinary property and cash at 90 calendar days and high-value non-cash property at 180 calendar days, then complete the approved disposal or suspense-account transfer under two-person control.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: log ordinary property.
- Duty Manager plus second authorised employee: sensitive/high-value custody and the physical found-cash recount, till drop or deposit handover.
- Security Lead: suspicious-item response and authority liaison.
- Finance Manager: issue the pre-numbered transfer reference, designate the deposit route, post the suspense-account entry and complete reconciliation.
- Any employee: immediate life-safety evacuation under OPS-009-4.11, OPS-003-4.5 and OPS-007-3.5 when credible danger exists; no prior approval is required.
- Finance Manager plus Loss Prevention: reconstructed found-cash amount where contemporaneous evidence cannot establish the amount; Finance Manager also reconciles any related suspense entry.

## Escalation rule

Treat wires, leakage, unusual odour, threats or deliberate concealment as suspicious. Credible danger triggers immediate evacuation under OPS-007/OPS-003; regional approval is not required before protecting people.

## Do not

- Do not search or move a suspicious bag.
- Do not publish photographs of found property.
- Do not release on a generic description.
- Do not access data on an unclaimed phone or computer.
- Do not record found cash as a sale, refund, void, petty-cash replenishment or till-variance offset.
- Do not silently remove, replace or estimate commingled cash, and do not continue using the affected container before dual-control reconciliation.
