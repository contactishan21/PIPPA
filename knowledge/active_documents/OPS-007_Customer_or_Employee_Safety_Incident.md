## When to use this document

Someone is injured, becomes unwell, or a hazardous event occurs on site.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-007-3.1

> Protect life first: call emergency services when urgent medical attention or immediate danger is present.

### OPS-007-3.2

> Only trained employees may provide first aid within the limits of their training.

### OPS-007-3.3

> The area and evidence must be preserved after immediate hazards are controlled.

### OPS-007-3.4

> An incident report must contain observed facts and must not speculate about blame.

### OPS-007-3.5

> Any employee may initiate evacuation and call emergency services for credible immediate danger. The Duty Manager leads when available, but their absence, incapacity or non-response must not delay life-safety action. A good-faith evacuation based on observed facts or an emergency-service, Security or alarm instruction is authorised; the initiating employee records the observed trigger and handover after reaching safety.

## Procedure

1. Make the area safe without creating further risk.
2. If credible immediate danger is observed or directed by emergency services, Security or an alarm, any employee may activate evacuation and call emergency services; the Duty Manager leads only when available without delay.
3. Call emergency services and a trained first aider as needed.
4. Notify the duty manager.
5. Record facts, witnesses and photographs where appropriate.
6. Escalate and follow up under the incident classification.

## Approval and decision authority

- Any employee: emergency call.
- Duty manager: site response.
- HSE director: serious injury, hospitalisation or regulator contact.
- Any employee: immediate evacuation for credible danger under OPS-007-3.5; no prior approval is required.

## Required records

- Time/location
- Observed facts
- Actions taken
- Witness details
- Incident reference
- Evacuation initiator and good-faith trigger
- Emergency-service/Security instruction and handover

## Escalation rule

Immediate HSE escalation for serious injury, fire, structural failure, violence, suspected contamination or regulator attendance.

## Do not

- Do not delay emergency assistance to complete paperwork.
- Do not admit liability or coach witnesses.
- Do not wait for the Duty Manager or regional approval where delay would increase an immediate life-safety risk.
