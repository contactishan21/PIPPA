## When to use this document

We want to set up a display stand outside the store.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### MKT-002-4.1

> Each in-store brand activation must obtain documented mall, property-management and public-authority permits where required for that location and activity before execution.

### MKT-002-4.2

> Third-party event staffing agencies must provide proof of valid worker health insurance and liability coverage before stepping onto the shop floor.

### MKT-002-4.3

> Do not obstruct fire exits, aisles, or POS queues with promotional stands or interactive displays.

### MKT-002-4.4

> Permits must be obtained from the mall, property manager or public authority only where required for the location and activation; Legal confirms uncertain requirements. Fourteen days is a planning target, not a substitute for a valid permit.

### MKT-002-4.5

> The event owner completes a safety and accessibility check, verifies insurance and contractor access, and maintains clear exits, aisles and queues throughout the activation.

### MKT-002-4.6

> Campaign spend and commitments follow FIN-001. A late permit, cancellation fee or fine may not be paid or concealed as ordinary event spend and must be escalated to Legal and Finance.
