# ECO-001 — Digital Gift Card Issuance Failure & Manual Override

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** E-commerce Manager
- **Audience:** Applies to digital gift card failures.
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

The customer bought an e-gift card but didn't receive the email.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### ECO-001-4.1

> Manual issuance authority is: E-commerce Manager up to AED 10,000; E-commerce Manager plus Commercial Director for AED 10,001–50,000.

### ECO-001-4.2

> Before resend or reissue, verify the purchaser and unique transaction under RET-003 controls and confirm card status, recipient address and delivery logs without disclosing account data.

### ECO-001-4.3

> Use resend before replacement where the original value remains valid. A replacement requires void or block of the original, duplicate check, linked references and two-person confirmation.

### ECO-001-4.4

> Manual issuance follows FIN-001 for all bands and OPS-005 controls; expiry, balance and terms may not be altered to solve a delivery failure.

## Procedure

4. Obtain each required functional and financial approval before commitment, release, payment, posting or disposal.
5. Record the decision, approvers, evidence, system references and closure outcome, then complete any required reconciliation or follow-up.
