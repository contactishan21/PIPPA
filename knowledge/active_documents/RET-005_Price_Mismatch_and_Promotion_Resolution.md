## When to use this document

Shelf, app, website or promotional messaging shows a different price at checkout.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### RET-005-4.1

> The displayed price, item identifier, promotion dates and eligibility conditions must be checked before any adjustment.

### RET-005-4.2

> A clear company pricing error may be honoured by a Duty Manager for all otherwise eligible units in one customer transaction when the total aggregated price difference is AED 500 or less and the applicable configured or default quantity limit in RET-005-4.7 is not exceeded, unless prohibited by law or suspected fraud.

### RET-005-4.3

> Expired, member-only or quantity-limited offers must be explained using the relevant condition, not blamed on ‘the system’.

### RET-005-4.4

> Repeated mismatch reports must be sent to Commercial Operations for correction.

### RET-005-4.5

> Price-remediation authority is: Duty Manager for one customer transaction with up to AED 500 aggregate difference within the quantity limit in RET-005-4.7; Commercial Director plus Finance Manager for AED 501–10,000 total customer exposure or any multiple-customer or campaign-wide correction up to AED 10,000; Chief Commercial Officer plus Finance Controller for AED 10,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### RET-005-4.6

> The full difference across every identical unit and any related same-event transaction must be calculated before approval. Duty Manager authority is based on aggregate financial exposure, not merely the number of units, but it does not waive the quantity limit in RET-005-4.7. An exposure above AED 500, or a multiple-customer or campaign-wide remedy, moves the entire correction to the applicable higher band; the exposure must not be split by unit, receipt, customer transaction or store.

### RET-005-4.7

> The applicable quantity limit is the item or promotion limit configured in the approved product or campaign record. If no limit is configured, the default is 10 identical units per customer in one calendar day, aggregated across the current transaction and any system-linked same-day transactions. A published or configured lower limit applies; a formally approved campaign limit above 10 replaces the default for that campaign. A request above the applicable limit requires Commercial Director operational approval plus the financial approvers in RET-005-4.5 for the full requested quantity and aggregate price difference before any correction; Commercial Director plus Finance Manager may approve only where total exposure does not exceed AED 10,000. Employees must not infer links between anonymous customers or divide a sale to avoid the limit.

## Procedure

1. Photograph or capture the displayed price and product code.
2. Check dates, channel, eligibility and the configured item or promotion quantity limit; if none exists, apply the default limit of 10 identical units per customer per calendar day in RET-005-4.7.
3. Calculate the total difference across every identical unit requested and any related same-event transaction so the full exposure is visible.
4. A Duty Manager may correct all otherwise eligible units in one customer transaction only where the aggregate difference is AED 500 or less.
5. If the requested quantity exceeds the applicable limit, obtain Commercial Director plus Finance Manager approval for the full quantity and aggregate difference before correcting any unit.
6. If the aggregate difference exceeds AED 500, or the remedy is multiple-customer or campaign-wide, request approval for the full exposure under RET-005-4.5.
7. Correct only the authorised units in the transaction.
8. Log the display defect for removal or digital correction.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: no discretionary override.
- Duty Manager: one customer transaction where the full aggregated difference is up to and including AED 500 and the quantity does not exceed the configured campaign limit or default 10-unit limit.
- Commercial Director plus Finance Manager: a quantity-limit exception only where the full aggregate exposure is up to and including AED 10,000; higher exposure also requires the higher financial approvers in RET-005-4.5, with Commercial Director retained as operational approver.
- Commercial Director plus Finance Manager: AED 501–10,000 total customer exposure, or any multiple-customer or campaign-wide correction up to AED 10,000.
- Chief Commercial Officer plus Finance Controller: AED 10,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000, in line with FIN-001.

## Escalation rule

Escalate suspected tampering, systemic or multiple-customer errors, breach of a normal quantity limit or an aggregate difference above AED 500.

## Do not

- Do not remove evidence before capturing it.
- Do not create an undocumented manual discount.
