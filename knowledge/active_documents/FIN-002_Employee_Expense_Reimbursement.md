# FIN-002 — Employee Expense Reimbursement

## Control information

- **Owner:** Finance Operations Director
- **Audience:** All employees and approving managers
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.10
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

An employee claims business travel, meals, transport or another out-of-pocket expense.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### FIN-002-4.1

> Expenses must be necessary, reasonable, business-related and supported by an itemised receipt.

### FIN-002-4.2

> Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days; older claims require manager explanation and Finance approval.

### FIN-002-4.3

> The claimant's direct or designated independent manager must approve; self-approval is prohibited.

### FIN-002-4.4

> Alcohol, fines, personal upgrades and companion costs are not reimbursable unless a specific written exception applies.

### FIN-002-4.5

> For exceptions, a Finance Manager may approve a missing receipt up to AED 500 or a late claim up to AED 10,000. A Finance Director may approve a missing receipt from AED 501-10,000 or a late claim from AED 10,001-50,000.

### FIN-002-4.6

> A missing-receipt claim above AED 10,000 requires independent alternative evidence of the supplier, date, amount, payment and business purpose. AED 10,001-50,000 requires Finance Director plus Internal Audit Director; AED 50,001-250,000 requires CFO plus Internal Audit Director; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO plus Internal Audit Director. Documentation-exception approval does not approve the underlying reimbursement: Line Manager validation and the separate transaction-value authority required by FIN-001 are also mandatory.

### FIN-002-4.7

> When one claim triggers more than one exception, including both a missing receipt and late submission, every evidence requirement applies and the claim must be approved at the highest and strictest authority required by any triggered exception. Approvals are cumulative where the exception types require different control functions; a lower approval for one exception cannot reduce the approval required for another.

### FIN-002-4.8

> The missing-receipt exception in FIN-002 applies only when an employee used personal funds and is seeking reimbursement. It must not be used to clear, reclassify or reimburse petty cash, a micro-advance or another company-funded cash advance issued under FIN-005; those items remain subject to FIN-005-4.7 cash-accountability controls.

### FIN-002-4.9

> An employee payment made to replace, top up, repay, conceal or balance a till shortage, petty-cash shortage, missing advance, unexplained company-cash balance or found-cash commingling is not a reimbursable business expense. Finance must reject such a claim, preserve the payment evidence and link it to the controlling OPS-002, OPS-009 or FIN-005 investigation. Voluntary tender of personal funds does not reduce the gross shortage, close the case or remove any write-off, Loss Prevention, Internal Audit or lawful-recovery requirement.

## Procedure

1. Confirm that the employee used personal funds; company cash issued under FIN-005 must remain in the FIN-005 reconciliation process.
2. Confirm that the claim is a genuine third-party business expense and not a payment used to replace, top up, repay, conceal or balance company cash under FIN-002-4.9.
3. Itemise expenses and business purpose.
4. Attach readable receipts and required travel evidence, or the required independent alternative evidence for a missing receipt.
5. Identify every triggered exception, including missing evidence, late submission and prohibited-category requests.
6. Apply every evidence requirement and route the claim to the highest strictest authority, retaining any additional control function required by another triggered exception.
7. Manager reviews necessity and conflicts.
8. Finance audits and reimburses through payroll/bank only after all approvals are complete.

## Approval and decision authority

- Line Manager: standard, timely claim with complete receipt.
- Finance Manager: missing-receipt exception up to and including AED 500; separately, a late-claim exception of any amount up to and including AED 10,000.
- Finance Director: missing-receipt exception from AED 501-10,000, or late claim from AED 10,001-50,000.
- Finance Director plus Internal Audit Director: missing-receipt exception from AED 10,001-50,000, supported by independent alternative evidence.
- CFO plus Internal Audit Director: missing-receipt exception from AED 50,001-250,000, supported by independent alternative evidence and the financial authority required by FIN-001.
- CFO plus CEO plus Internal Audit Director: missing-receipt exception above AED 250,000, supported by independent alternative evidence.
- Every exception approval is additional to the Line Manager's business validation and the transaction-value approval required by FIN-001.

## Required records

- Receipt or independent alternative evidence
- Business purpose
- Attendees where relevant
- All triggered exception types
- Strictest authority calculation
- Line Manager validation
- Documentation-exception approval
- FIN-001 transaction-value approval
- Exception reason
- Company-cash replacement screening result
- Linked cash incident and preserved payment evidence where rejected

## Escalation rule

Escalate suspected duplicate, altered receipt, personal spend or manager/claimant collusion.

## Do not

- Do not split one expense across claims.
- Do not approve each exception separately and then use the lower result.
- Do not use an employee-expense exception to clear petty cash, a micro-advance or another company-funded cash advance.
- Do not approve your own claim.
- Do not claim recoverable VAT without valid evidence.
- Do not reimburse personal funds used to balance a till, safe, petty-cash tin, advance or found-cash incident, even if the employee first paid the money into company custody.
