## Authoritative policy clauses

### COM-001-4.1

> Unauthorized employees, including Store Managers and Duty Managers, must not issue statements, confirm facts, or provide commentary to media personnel.

### COM-001-4.2

> All media inquiries must be redirected to the PR & Communications Director immediately.

### COM-001-4.3

> In the event of a severe reputational incident (e.g., safety breach, viral negative press), the Crisis Management Team (CMT) assumes sole control of internal and external communications.

### COM-001-4.4

> A severe incident includes fatality, serious injury, public-safety threat, material data breach, regulator action, major service outage or rapidly escalating high-reach coverage. Employees escalate uncertainty rather than classify it down.

### COM-001-4.5

> Outside normal hours, employees use the published crisis contact. If immediate acknowledgement is unavoidable, only the approved holding statement may be used; it must not confirm unverified facts.

### COM-001-4.6

> Employees must preserve messages, recordings and screenshots, must not post personal commentary about the incident, and must protect confidential and personal information.
