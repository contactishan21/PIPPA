# PRC-009 — Approved Vendor Use and Contracting

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

A team needs a supplier and asks whether it must use an approved vendor or sign a contract.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### PRC-009-4.1

> Approved framework vendors must be used where their scope, capacity and commercial terms meet the requirement.

### PRC-009-4.2

> A purchase order is not a substitute for a signed contract when services involve on-site work, personal data, intellectual property, vehicles, subcontractors, material liability or a term longer than three months.

### PRC-009-4.3

> Only authorised signatories may bind the company; email acceptance by a requestor is not contract approval.

### PRC-009-4.4

> Vendor selection and contract status must be recorded before service starts.

## Procedure

1. Search the approved vendor register by category and location.
2. Confirm capacity, scope and pricing route.
3. Identify legal, insurance, data, safety and operational risks.
4. Use the approved contract template and specialist reviews.
5. Obtain signatures and PO before mobilisation.

## Approval and decision authority

- Procurement manager: approved-vendor selection.
- Legal: contract terms and exceptions.
- Authorised signatory under FIN-001: signature.
- CPO: non-approved vendor exception.

## Required records

- Vendor-register result
- Risk review
- Contract
- Signature authority
- PO

## Escalation rule

Escalate requests to start work before signature, use vendor terms only, or engage an unapproved subcontractor.

## Do not

- Do not sign vendor documents without authority.
- Do not treat an approved-vendor label as approval for every scope.
