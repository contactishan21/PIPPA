# RET-001 — Returns, Refunds and Exchanges

## When to use this document

A customer asks to return or exchange a product, with or without the original receipt.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### RET-001-4.1

> A standard return may be accepted within 30 calendar days of purchase when the item is unused, resalable and supported by valid proof of purchase.

### RET-001-4.2

> Refunds must be made to the original payment method. Where that method cannot technically receive the refund, a duty manager may approve a credit note.

### RET-001-4.3

> Defective, unsafe or incorrectly supplied goods are not rejected only because the standard 30-day return window has passed; RET-002 must be followed.

### RET-001-4.4

> Personalised goods, opened hygiene-sensitive goods and activated digital products are not eligible for change-of-mind return unless defective.

### RET-001-4.5

> Standard remedy authority is: Associate up to AED 1,000 and Duty Manager for AED 1,001–5,000 under configured POS controls; Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 5,001–50,000; Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000. A system-issued credit note follows the same bands.

### RET-001-4.6

> An exchange must be processed as a return of the original item and a linked new purchase within one POS transaction. Return eligibility and approval authority are determined by the gross value of the returned item or items, not by the net price difference after the replacement purchase.

### RET-001-4.7

> Where the replacement costs more, the customer pays the additional amount through the normal payment flow. Where it costs less, the balance is returned to the original payment method or issued as an authorised credit note. The two linked legs and the approving authority must remain visible in the POS audit trail.

### RET-001-4.8

> RET-001 does not authorise cancellation or refund of an uncollected high-value, age-restricted or flagged online order as a way to bypass OPS-006 identity controls. Such a request follows OPS-006-4.8: refund release requires approved step-up verification and may only be an automatic reversal to the original payment method. A credit note, cash, different payment method or manual override is prohibited while identity remains unverified.

## Procedure

1. Identify the transaction using the receipt, order number, payment reference or loyalty account.
2. Confirm purchase date, selling channel, item condition and reason for return or exchange.
3. Apply the standard rule or route defects to RET-002.
4. Explain the available outcome in plain language before processing it.
5. For an exchange, return the original item and create the replacement as a linked new purchase within one POS transaction; calculate authority from the gross value of the returned item or items, not the net price difference.
6. Collect any customer top-up through the normal payment flow or return any lower-price balance through the authorised refund or credit-note method.
7. Record the return or exchange reason and evidence in the returns log, then issue a receipt showing both linked legs or the case number.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: compliant standard returns up to and including AED 1,000 under configured POS controls.
- Duty Manager: AED 1,001–5,000 and system-issued credit-note exceptions within that band under configured POS controls.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: AED 5,001–50,000.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.

## Required records

- Transaction identifier
- Return or exchange reason and item condition
- Gross returned value
- Replacement sale reference
- Approval record where required
- Refund or credit-note reference

## Escalation rule

Pause and ask the duty manager if proof is inconclusive, the customer alleges a safety issue, or the refund exceeds the associate limit.

## Do not

- Do not promise cash when the original purchase was paid by card.
- Do not refuse a claimed defect solely because 30 days have passed.
- Do not process an exchange as an unlinked price adjustment or use the net difference to reduce approval authority.
- Do not copy full card numbers into notes.
