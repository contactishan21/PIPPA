# OPS-002 — Cash Variance Investigation

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Retail Finance Controller
- **Audience:** Cashiers, supervisors, duty managers
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.10
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

A till or safe count differs from the system total.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-002-4.1

> Every variance must be recorded; a variance is not corrected by changing sales data or adding personal money.

### OPS-002-4.2

> A recount must be completed under dual control before investigation.

### OPS-002-4.3

> Single variances above AED 200, repeated variances or evidence of tampering require same-shift escalation.

### OPS-002-4.4

> Employees must be treated fairly and must not be accused before evidence is reviewed.

### OPS-002-4.5

> Operational investigation authority does not include authority to post, clear or write off a variance in the ledger. A Supervisor may investigate and recommend an outcome up to AED 50, but may not independently execute the financial adjustment.

### OPS-002-4.6

> After investigation, financial write-off authority is: Duty Manager plus an independent Finance checker up to AED 200; Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 201-10,000; Regional Operations Director plus Finance Manager for AED 10,001-50,000; VP/COO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001-250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000. Loss Prevention review is additionally required above AED 200, for repeated variances or where tampering is suspected.

### OPS-002-4.7

> The Loss Prevention trigger in OPS-002-4.6 is the minimum company-cash loss standard and also applies to unsupported petty-cash, micro-advance and operational-advance balances under FIN-005-4.8. A different cash channel must not be used to reduce investigation or review requirements.

### OPS-002-4.8

> A personal cash top-up, repayment or loan does not correct or reduce a till or safe variance. The gross variance must be recorded before any employee payment and must follow OPS-002-4.6. Personal money already added must be removed from usable company cash under dual control, placed in a separately referenced suspense record and referred to Finance and HR or Legal; it cannot be reimbursed under FIN-002. Any employee recovery requires a documented finding and lawful recovery decision and does not replace Loss Prevention review.

## Procedure

1. Stop further use of the till where practical.
2. Recount cash and vouchers with a second employee.
3. Record the gross variance before accepting any personal funds. If money was already added, remove it from usable cash under dual control into a separate suspense record and refer it to Finance and HR or Legal under OPS-002-4.8.
4. Compare refunds, voids, drops and manual receipts.
5. Record the unexplained variance and separate the investigation finding from the proposed accounting treatment.
6. Obtain the operational and independent financial approvals required by OPS-002-4.6 before any ledger entry.
7. Preserve the approval, posting reference and evidence.

## Approval and decision authority

- Supervisor: investigate and recommend an outcome up to AED 50; no ledger-posting authority.
- Duty Manager: investigate AED 51-200 and co-approve a write-off up to AED 200 with an independent Finance checker.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: write-off from AED 201-10,000 after required Loss Prevention review.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Manager: AED 10,001-50,000.
- VP/COO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001-250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.
- Loss Prevention: mandatory review above AED 200, for repeated variances or suspected tampering.

## Required records

- Till ID
- Expected and actual total
- Recount names
- Relevant transactions
- Investigation finding
- Write-off approval
- Ledger posting reference
- Gross variance before personal tender
- Personal-funds suspense and Finance/HR or Legal referral
- Linked FIN-002 rejected claim where applicable

## Escalation rule

Contact loss prevention immediately for tampering, coercion, missing deposit bags or suspected theft.

## Do not

- Do not replace shortages with personal cash.
- Do not alter transaction records.
- Do not treat investigation authority as write-off authority.
- Do not discuss allegations publicly.
- Do not accept a personal top-up, treat it as correction of the variance or support a FIN-002 reimbursement claim for it.
