## When to use this document

The bank statement doesn't match the store deposits.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### FIN-006-4.1

> Authority to write off unrecoverable unmatched deposits is: Finance Manager plus Regional Operations Manager up to AED 10,000; Finance Director plus Head of Operations for AED 10,001–50,000.

### FIN-006-4.2

> Finance must trace each unmatched deposit to bank reference, store, tender, date, deposit bag and ledger entry; the item remains open and separately aged until matched or formally written off.

### FIN-006-4.3

> Write-off follows FIN-001 for all bands, including Finance Director plus Head of Operations for AED 10,001–50,000, VP/COO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000, and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### FIN-006-4.4

> Loss Prevention reviews suspected theft, tampering or repeated store patterns immediately; Internal Audit reviews a single-store rolling-month total above AED 5,000. Investigation does not authorise posting.

## Approval and decision authority

- The operational authority stated in the clauses governs the functional decision.
- Any spend, refund, credit, loss, write-off, settlement or commitment also requires the applicable FIN-001 financial approval; the stricter cumulative combination applies.
- No approver may self-approve, split related transactions or treat investigation, validation or proof acceptance as financial approval.

## Escalation rule

Escalate to Internal Audit if unmatched deposits from a single store exceed AED 5,000 in a month.
