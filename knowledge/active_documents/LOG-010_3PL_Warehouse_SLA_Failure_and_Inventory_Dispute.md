## When to use this document

The 3PL warehouse lost our stock.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-010-4.1

> Financial settlement authority for 3PL claims is: Head of Logistics plus Finance Manager up to AED 50,000; VP/COO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### LOG-010-4.2

> The incident owner preserves inventory records, scans, CCTV where lawfully available, seals, access logs and SLA evidence, and performs a joint quantity and custody reconciliation with the 3PL.

### LOG-010-4.3

> Settlement authority is additional to FIN-001 and is based on gross confirmed loss before expected insurance, carrier or 3PL recovery; related losses are aggregated.

### LOG-010-4.4

> A disputed settlement is not closed as a write-off. Legal, Finance and Logistics separately record liability, recovery, accounting treatment and corrective action.

## Approval and decision authority

- The operational authority stated in the clauses governs the functional decision.
- Any spend, refund, credit, loss, write-off, settlement or commitment also requires the applicable FIN-001 financial approval; the stricter cumulative combination applies.
- No approver may self-approve, split related transactions or treat investigation, validation or proof acceptance as financial approval.
