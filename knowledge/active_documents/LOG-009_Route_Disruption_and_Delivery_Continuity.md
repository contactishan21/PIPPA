# LOG-009 — Route Disruption and Delivery Continuity

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Supply Chain Control Tower Lead
- **Audience:** Drivers, dispatchers, fulfilment and customer service
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.2
- **Effective:** 03 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

Flooding, road closure, civil disruption, vehicle breakdown or another event interrupts deliveries.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-009-4.1

> Driver and public safety take priority over route completion or on-time metrics.

### LOG-009-4.2

> The control tower must classify the event, identify impacted orders and select an approved continuity option.

### LOG-009-4.3

> Customers must receive accurate, consistent updates without speculation.

### LOG-009-4.4

> Perishable, medical or high-value goods require product-specific custody decisions.

### LOG-009-4.5

> Tier 1 Local/Routine applies when no more than 10 planned routes are materially prevented from operating, expected duration is below two hours, impact is limited to one facility or delivery zone, and there is no immediate safety, regulatory, cold-chain or material customer-data risk. The Control Tower Lead owns Tier 1.

### LOG-009-4.6

> Tier 2 Regional/Major applies when 11–50 planned routes are materially prevented from operating, expected duration is two to eight hours, one city or emirate is significantly disrupted, or there is material backlog, perishable-goods exposure or repeated delivery failure. The Regional Logistics Director owns Tier 2 with the Control Tower Lead and must notify Customer Experience and affected fulfilment sites.

### LOG-009-4.7

> Tier 3 Network/Critical applies when more than 50 planned routes are materially prevented from operating, expected duration exceeds eight hours, multiple cities, emirates or fulfilment sites are affected, or there is government closure, major safety risk, loss of fleet contact, sensitive-goods exposure, media attention or significant reputational risk. The Supply Chain Director owns Tier 3 and activates the Crisis Management Team when a crisis trigger is present.

### LOG-009-4.8

> Where more than one classification trigger applies, the highest tier governs. A safety, government, regulatory or sensitive-goods trigger immediately requires Tier 3 regardless of route count. Only the owner of the current tier may approve a downgrade, and the classification, trigger, owner, communications and downgrade or closure decision must be recorded.

## Procedure

1. Move to a safe location and notify dispatch.
2. Count planned routes materially prevented from operating and estimate duration, geography and risk triggers.
3. Assign the highest applicable tier; do not average competing triggers downward.
4. Identify impacted orders and special-handling goods.
5. Reroute, recover, hold or return using the approved option.
6. Send the controlled internal and customer communications required for the tier.
7. Record the trigger, owner and decisions; only the current-tier owner may approve downgrade or closure.
8. Close with custody and incident reconciliation.

## Approval and decision authority

- Control Tower Lead: Tier 1 Local/Routine.
- Regional Logistics Director with Control Tower Lead: Tier 2 Regional/Major.
- Supply Chain Director: Tier 3 Network/Critical.
- Crisis Management Team: activated for Tier 3 crisis triggers including life safety, government action or significant reputation risk.

## Required records

- Event timeline
- Affected planned-route count
- Duration and geography
- Classification triggers and tier
- Decision owner
- Impacted orders
- Routing/custody decision
- Customer/internal messages
- Downgrade or closure approval
- Custody reconciliation

## Escalation rule

Immediately classify as Tier 3 for safety, government, regulatory or sensitive-goods triggers, regardless of route count. Activate the Crisis Management Team when a crisis trigger is present.

## Do not

- Do not pressure drivers to enter unsafe areas.
- Do not invent arrival times.
- Do not classify by delayed-order count; use planned routes materially prevented from operating.
- Do not downgrade an event without the current-tier owner's approval.
