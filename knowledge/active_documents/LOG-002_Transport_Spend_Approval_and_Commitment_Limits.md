## When to use this document

A logistics team needs to know the approval level for a transport commitment.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-002-4.1

> Approval is based on total expected commitment, including fuel surcharges, tolls, standby, extensions and taxes.

### LOG-002-4.2

> Up to AED 10,000 requires a Logistics Manager and independent Budget Owner or Finance check; AED 10,001–50,000 requires the Logistics Director, Procurement Manager and Finance Manager.

### LOG-002-4.3

> AED 50,001–250,000 requires COO, CPO and Finance Controller approval; above AED 250,000 requires CFO and CEO approval.

### LOG-002-4.4

> A commitment must not be divided by day, route, branch or invoice to remain below a limit.

### LOG-002-4.5

> Transport authority is: Logistics Manager plus independent Budget Owner or Finance check up to AED 10,000; Logistics Director plus Procurement Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 10,001–50,000; COO plus CPO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### LOG-002-4.6

> A renewal, extension or replacement order serving the same or substantially related transport requirement is aggregated with the original commitment when it was planned, reasonably foreseeable or known before expiry. The controlled continuity extension in LOG-001-5.7 is a variation to the original commitment, not a separate purchase, only when it is approved before expiry using the aggregated total and retains the same procurement record. Opening a new order, route, supplier record or seven-day period to reset duration or value is prohibited splitting.

## Procedure

1. Estimate total term and all likely charges.
2. Aggregate any planned, foreseeable or known renewal, extension or replacement serving the same requirement, including a LOG-001 continuity extension.
3. Confirm budget and sourcing route.
4. Submit one approval for the full commitment.
5. Create the PO/contract after approval.
6. Monitor actual spend and obtain approval before exceeding the approved amount.

## Approval and decision authority

- Up to and including AED 10,000: Logistics Manager plus independent Budget Owner/Finance check.
- AED 10,001–50,000: Logistics Director plus Procurement Manager plus Finance Manager.
- AED 50,001–250,000: COO plus CPO plus Finance Controller.
- Above AED 250,000: CFO plus CEO.

## Required records

- Cost model
- Budget code
- Approval
- PO/contract
- Variation approval
- Related commitment references
- Extension or renewal aggregation assessment

## Escalation rule

Escalate when total cost is uncertain, rates are open-ended or activation has already occurred.

## Do not

- Do not split commitments.
- Do not approve an open-ended ‘as incurred’ cost without a cap.
- Do not reset duration or value by opening a new supplier, route or order record for the same continuing requirement.
