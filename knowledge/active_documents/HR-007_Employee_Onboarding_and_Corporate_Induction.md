## Authoritative policy clauses

### HR-007-4.1

> All new hires must complete the standard corporate induction program within their first 14 calendar days of employment.

### HR-007-4.2

> System credentials, including POS and the approved inventory system access, will not be fully provisioned until mandatory compliance and information security modules are marked complete by HR.

### HR-007-4.3

> HR Operations must publish the mandatory induction-module list and the accountable module owner; completion evidence must be retained in the approved HCM or learning system.

### HR-007-4.4

> Temporary and agency workers must complete the role-appropriate safety, security, privacy and site induction before access; the sponsoring manager owns this alternate route.

### HR-007-4.5

> Only the minimum access needed for induction may be issued before certification. Production, POS, payment, inventory and privileged access remains blocked until HR and the system owner record completion.
