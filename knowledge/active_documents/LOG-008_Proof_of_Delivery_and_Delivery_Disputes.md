# LOG-008 — Proof of Delivery and Delivery Disputes

## When to use this document

A delivery is completed or a customer disputes receipt.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-008-4.1

> Proof of delivery must capture the authorised recipient, date/time, location event and item status using the approved application.

### LOG-008-4.2

> Signatures or photos must avoid unnecessary personal or household information.

### LOG-008-4.3

> A disputed delivery requires preservation of GPS, scan, contact and custody evidence.

### LOG-008-4.4

> A driver must not create or alter proof after leaving the location except through a documented correction workflow.

### LOG-008-4.5

> For delivery disputes, high-value loss means aggregated goods value of AED 5,000 or more across related orders or one custody event. Such a case requires the Last-Mile Manager and Loss Prevention review before a loss finding or customer remedy is approved. Customer communication and any time-critical temporary support may continue, but the final refund, replacement, credit note, write-off and carrier claim follow the applicable RET, LOG and FIN authority without netting or splitting.

### LOG-008-4.6

> LOG-008 governs the customer-facing non-receipt dispute and proof-of-delivery review. When records show that goods disappeared before customer handover or no valid proof of delivery exists and the carrier cannot locate them, LOG-006-4.7 and LOG-006-4.8 govern the custody investigation, inventory-loss decision, write-off and carrier claim. Both cases must share one incident reference; closing the customer dispute does not close the custody investigation, and closing the custody investigation does not itself authorise a customer remedy.

## Procedure

1. Verify recipient/collection instruction.
2. Capture approved POD elements.
3. Close the stop in real time.
4. For a dispute, preserve evidence, aggregate related goods value and open a case.
5. If evidence places the loss before customer handover, open or link the LOG-006 custody investigation under the same incident reference; continue the customer-facing dispute separately.
6. If value is AED 5,000 or more, obtain the mandatory Loss Prevention review in LOG-008-4.5 before the final loss finding or remedy.
7. Customer service explains the investigation timing and authorised interim options.

## Approval and decision authority

- Dispatcher: correct obvious system error.
- Last-Mile Manager: disputed delivery investigation.
- Loss Prevention: every aggregated loss of AED 5,000 or more, suspected falsification or repeated pattern.
- Privacy: personal-data incident.

## Required records

- POD
- GPS/scan
- Custody record
- Contact log
- Correction audit trail
- Linked LOG-006 custody incident
- Custody-loss decision status

## Escalation rule

Escalate suspected falsification, high-value loss, repeated driver pattern or disclosure of private information.

## Do not

- Do not sign for a customer.
- Do not photograph children, identity documents or inside a home.
