## When to use this document

What do I do with an online order that hasn't been picked up?

## Authoritative policy clauses

### RET-007-4.1

> Uncollected orders must be held in a secure staging area for exactly 14 calendar days.

### RET-007-4.2

> On day 15, the Duty Manager must systematically cancel the order in the approved inventory system to trigger the customer refund and return the items to stock.

### RET-007-4.3

> Collection release follows OPS-006 identity rules, including aggregation of related orders and the high-value original-ID requirement.

### RET-007-4.4

> The customer must receive the approved reminder schedule before cancellation. Perishable, age-restricted and otherwise controlled goods follow their stricter handling and shorter hold rules.

### RET-007-4.5

> Cancellation and refund require an unused-order check, dual-control confirmation and the original payment method. A failed or disputed refund is held and escalated; goods are not released as a workaround.
