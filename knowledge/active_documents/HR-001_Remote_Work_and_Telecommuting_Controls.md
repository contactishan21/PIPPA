## Authoritative policy clauses

### HR-001-4.1

> Remote work requires prior written approval and a secure internet connection. Working from public unencrypted Wi-Fi networks is prohibited without a company VPN.

### HR-001-4.2

> Remote work approval authority is: Line Manager for up to 2 days per week; Department Director plus HR Director for full-time remote or cross-border remote work exceeding 30 days.

### HR-001-4.3

> Remote work of three or four days per week requires Department Director plus HR Director approval. Cross-border remote work of any duration also requires Information Security and Legal review; periods over 30 days additionally require Department Director plus HR Director approval.

### HR-001-4.4

> The employee must use company-managed devices, the approved VPN and approved storage. Suspected loss, compromise or unauthorised access must be reported to IT Security immediately.

### HR-001-4.5

> Approval records must state location, days, start and end dates, data-access restrictions and review date; approval does not create a permanent contractual entitlement.

## Approval and decision authority

- The operational authority stated in the clauses governs the functional decision.
- Any spend, refund, credit, loss, write-off, settlement or commitment also requires the applicable FIN-001 financial approval; the stricter cumulative combination applies.
- No approver may self-approve, split related transactions or treat investigation, validation or proof acceptance as financial approval.
