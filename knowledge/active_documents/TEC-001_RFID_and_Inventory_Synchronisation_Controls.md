## Authoritative policy clauses

### TEC-001-4.1

> System-driven blind cycle counts must be executed weekly. Discrepancies between physical RFID scans and the approved inventory system master data must undergo a documented root-cause review if the variance exceeds 2%.

### TEC-001-4.2

> Operational write-offs resulting from the approved inventory system inventory synchronization variances follow FIN-001: Duty Manager up to AED 1,000; Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 1,001–50,000.

### TEC-001-4.3

> The Inventory Control Manager owns root-cause review for variance above 2%, assigns corrective action within two business days and records closure; no named improvement methodology is mandatory.

### TEC-001-4.4

> While synchronisation is unavailable, movements must use the approved outage log and dual control, then be reconciled before normal processing resumes.

### TEC-001-4.5

> Investigation and correction do not themselves authorise a write-off. Every ledger write-off follows FIN-001 and any stricter loss-prevention control.
