## When to use this document

How do we send bad stock back to the warehouse?

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-013-4.1

> All RTV shipments must be systematically processed through the approved inventory system with a 100% RFID outbound scan to ensure inventory master data integrity.

### LOG-013-4.2

> Defective, recalled, or out-of-season stock must not sit in the store backroom for more than 14 calendar days before being consolidated and dispatched to the central reverse logistics hub.

### LOG-013-4.3

> Before dispatch, segregate the stock, determine whether the cause is supplier, carrier, store, recall or warranty, and obtain the supplier RMA or approved hub instruction.

### LOG-013-4.4

> Use the approved reverse-logistics route and an outbound scan for every scannable unit; an approved manual manifest and later reconciliation is required where scanning is unavailable.

### LOG-013-4.5

> The receiving hub confirms quantity and condition. Supplier credit, carrier claim and inventory write-off are separate records; LOG-006 applies to carrier damage and FIN-001 to any write-off.

### LOG-013-4.6

> Dispatch after 14 days requires Warehouse Manager approval, documented reason and a revised collection date; unsafe or recalled stock remains quarantined regardless of timing.
