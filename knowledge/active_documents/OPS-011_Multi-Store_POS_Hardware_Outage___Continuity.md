## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-011-4.1

> Emergency hardware replacement or IT vendor dispatch authority is: Head of Operations plus IT Director up to AED 50,000; VP/COO plus IT Director for AED 50,001–250,000.

### OPS-011-4.2

> A widespread outage affects two or more stores or a shared regional service; the IT Incident Manager leads technical recovery and the Operations Incident Lead controls trading continuity.

### OPS-011-4.3

> Stores follow OPS-001 for manual trading, payment-data restrictions and reconciliation. No emergency replacement permits staff to record card details for later entry.

### OPS-011-4.4

> Emergency cost authority follows FIN-001, including CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000, in addition to the operational and IT approvals stated here.
