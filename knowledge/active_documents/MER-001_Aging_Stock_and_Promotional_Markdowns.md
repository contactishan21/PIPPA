## When to use this document

## Authoritative policy clauses

### MER-001-4.1

> Markdowns must be applied centrally through the master pricing system.

### MER-001-4.2

> Authority to approve the aggregate total margin reduction for a markdown campaign is: Brand Manager or Merchandising Manager up to AED 10,000; Brand Manager plus Commercial Director for AED 10,001–50,000; VP/COO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### MER-001-4.3

> Aggregate margin reduction is the total forecast reduction across all included SKUs, stores, channels and campaign dates against the approved baseline margin; related waves are combined.

### MER-001-4.4

> Either the authorised Brand Manager or Merchandising Manager may initiate approval up to AED 10,000; neither may approve their own request without an independent review.

### MER-001-4.5

> Campaign approval is additional to FIN-001 financial approval. Price-file upload requires maker-checker control, effective dates, test evidence and post-launch reconciliation.
