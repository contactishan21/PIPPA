# RET-002 — Defective Item Inspection and Resolution

## When to use this document

A customer returns a defective item after the normal return window—for example, after 38 days.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### RET-002-5.1

> When a customer reports a defect after the standard return window, the associate must first identify the transaction and then arrange a product inspection; the return must not be declined solely because it is late.

### RET-002-5.2

> The associate must clearly state that the final remedy depends on inspection and must not promise a refund, replacement or credit note before the defect is confirmed.

### RET-002-5.3

> The customer's preferred remedy—same-item replacement, refund to the original payment method, or credit note—must be recorded at intake and considered after inspection.

### RET-002-5.4

> Inspection outcomes must be communicated within two business days for store-stock items and five business days for items sent to a specialist centre.

### RET-002-5.5

> The Inspection Lead confirms the technical defect and the Regional Customer Resolution Manager owns a disputed finding. Financial remedies then follow RET-001 and FIN-004: Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 5,001–50,000; Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

## Procedure

1. Acknowledge the customer and find the transaction through the loyalty number, receipt, order number or payment reference.
2. Confirm that the item is beyond the 30-day standard window and explain that a defect review still applies.
3. Ask the customer to describe the fault; photograph the item and record serial/batch details where relevant.
4. Tell the customer: ‘Because the item may be defective, our duty manager or inspection team needs to check it before we confirm the remedy.’
5. Ask which outcome they would prefer if the defect is confirmed: replacement, refund to the original payment method, or credit note.
6. Issue an inspection receipt and give the expected response time.
7. After inspection, contact the customer, explain the finding and process the approved remedy.

## Approval and decision authority

- Duty Manager: accepts item for local inspection.
- Inspection Lead: confirms the technical defect; the resulting remedy may be authorised up to and including AED 5,000 under RET-001.
- Regional Customer Resolution Manager: resolves disputed inspection findings.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: financial remedy from AED 5,001–50,000.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.

## Required records

- Transaction and loyalty reference
- Customer fault description
- Images and serial/batch number
- Preferred remedy
- Inspection finding and approval
- Customer communication timestamp

## Escalation rule

Escalate immediately for overheating, electrical shock, injury, contamination, recall indicators or repeated defects from the same batch.

## Do not

- Do not tell the customer ‘the policy says no’ merely because 30 days have passed.
- Do not imply that inspection guarantees a refund.
- Do not attempt unsafe product testing on the shop floor.
