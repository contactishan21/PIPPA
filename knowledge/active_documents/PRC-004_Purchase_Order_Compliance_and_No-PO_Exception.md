# PRC-004 — Purchase Order Compliance and No-PO Exception

## When to use this document

Work was requested or completed without an approved purchase order.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### PRC-004-4.1

> A purchase order must be approved before a vendor commits cost or starts work, except for documented emergency categories.

### PRC-004-4.2

> A retrospective PO does not erase the control failure and requires a No-PO Exception Declaration.

### PRC-004-4.3

> The approver must validate price, receipt, budget and conflict of interest before regularising the transaction.

### PRC-004-4.4

> Repeated exceptions are reported to the relevant director and Procurement Governance.

### PRC-004-4.5

> A No-PO exception up to AED 50,000 requires Department Director plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 requires CPO plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO. These approvals regularise the exception but do not erase the control failure.

### PRC-004-4.6

> A missed PRC-007 emergency-approval documentation deadline triggers the late-emergency regularisation process in PRC-007-5.6. If the emergency commitment also lacked an approved purchase order before cost was committed, a No-PO Exception Declaration and every approval in PRC-004-4.5 are additionally required. Completing one process does not satisfy the other, and neither may be backdated.

## Procedure

1. Stop further unapproved work where safe.
2. Collect scope, quote, completion evidence and reason for bypass.
3. Raise a No-PO Exception Declaration.
4. For a late-recorded emergency, also raise the separate PRC-007-5.6 regularisation; do not treat either record as satisfying the other.
5. Obtain the approval that would have applied before commitment and the exception authority in PRC-004-4.5.
6. Procurement creates the controlled PO or declines regularisation.
7. AP pays only after PO, receipt and invoice match or an approved invoice-substitute process.

## Approval and decision authority

- Department Director plus Finance Manager: up to and including AED 50,000; this is stricter than the lowest FIN-001 band because every No-PO case is an exception.
- CPO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000, as required by FIN-001. Retrospective status never reduces the original approval level.

## Required records

- Exception declaration
- Scope and quote
- Evidence of receipt
- Approval
- Corrective action

## Escalation rule

Escalate deliberate bypass, split orders, personal relationships or unsupported completion claims.

## Do not

- Do not backdate a PO.
- Do not split invoices.
- Do not ask a vendor to change dates to fit controls.
