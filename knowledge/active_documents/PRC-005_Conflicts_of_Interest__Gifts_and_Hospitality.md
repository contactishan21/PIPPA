# PRC-005 — Conflicts of Interest, Gifts and Hospitality

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Ethics & Compliance Officer
- **Audience:** All employees involved in vendor decisions
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.10
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

An employee has a personal connection to a vendor or is offered a gift, meal, travel or event access.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### PRC-005-3.1

> Actual, potential or perceived conflicts must be declared before participating in a vendor decision.

### PRC-005-3.2

> Cash, cash equivalents and personal discounts from vendors are prohibited.

### PRC-005-3.3

> Modest business hospitality may be accepted only when legitimate, transparent, outside every prohibited decision period and within the event-frequency and rolling-value limits in PRC-005-3.8. Every offer must still be registered under PRC-005-3.7, including an accepted event of AED 300 or less.

### PRC-005-3.4

> No gift or hospitality may be accepted during an active tender or negotiation.

### PRC-005-3.5

> A single vendor-funded hospitality event from AED 301 through AED 1,000 per person requires Compliance Officer plus Function Director approval before acceptance; above AED 1,000 requires Chief Legal Officer plus the relevant Executive Committee member. Every offer is entered in the Ethics Register. Repeated lower-value events and cumulative totals follow PRC-005-3.8. No approval can override an active tender, negotiation, award, dispute or other prohibited decision period.

### PRC-005-3.6

> No hospitality exception is permitted during an active tender, vendor selection, contract negotiation, dispute or renewal decision. Cash, cash equivalents, personal travel, companion costs and personal entertainment cannot be approved as exceptions. Where attendance at an industry event has a legitimate business purpose, the preferred approach is for the company to purchase admission directly.

### PRC-005-3.7

> Every vendor-funded meal, ticket, event, travel offer, hospitality benefit and gift must be entered in the central Ethics Register whether accepted, declined or transferred and regardless of value. The entry is made before acceptance when approval is required, otherwise within two business days. Ordinary refreshments available to all attendees at a vendor workplace or open event may be recorded once by the meeting owner with the attendee list. Value is recorded per recipient at the higher of stated cost or reasonable market value; related benefits must not be divided by event, attendee, invoice or host.

### PRC-005-3.8

> Hospitality is infrequent only where the same vendor provides no more than two events to the same employee in a rolling 90-calendar-day period and the aggregate value does not exceed AED 300. The third event, or any event causing the rolling 90-day total to exceed AED 300, requires Compliance Officer plus Function Director approval before acceptance even when each event is AED 300 or less. The event that causes the same vendor's rolling 12-month total to exceed AED 1,000, and every later event while that rolling total remains above AED 1,000, requires Chief Legal Officer plus the relevant Executive Committee member before acceptance. Active-decision prohibitions in PRC-005-3.4 and PRC-005-3.6 still apply and cannot be approved as exceptions.

### PRC-005-3.9

> A non-cash vendor gift may be accepted personally only when it is unsolicited, branded or customary, has a market value of AED 100 or less, is outside every prohibited decision period and does not cause the same vendor's rolling 90-day gift total to the employee to exceed AED 100. All gifts are registered under PRC-005-3.7. A gift above either limit must be declined or returned; where return is impractical, the Compliance Officer may direct transfer to the company for shared use, donation or documented disposal, but may not approve personal retention. Cash and cash equivalents remain prohibited at every value.

## Procedure

1. Decline prohibited or tender-period offers politely.
2. Enter every offer in the Ethics Register, whether accepted, declined or transferred. Calculate market value per recipient and the same-vendor rolling 90-day event count/value and rolling 12-month value before acceptance.
3. Apply the single-event and cumulative hospitality approvals in PRC-005-3.5 and PRC-005-3.8, and apply the separate gift limit and disposition route in PRC-005-3.9.
4. For hospitality above AED 300, record the event, organiser, vendor relationship, actual value, business purpose and expected attendees before acceptance.
5. Confirm that no tender, selection, negotiation, dispute or renewal decision is active.
6. Use company-paid admission where practical; otherwise obtain the required advance approval.
7. Declare the relationship or offer in the ethics register and record the decision.
8. Pause decision participation if independence could reasonably be questioned.
9. Record disposal or return of an impermissible gift.

## Approval and decision authority

- Line Manager: routine declaration.
- Compliance Officer: recusal, gift and hospitality exceptions within the expressly stated limits; no authority to waive absolute prohibitions.
- Compliance Officer plus Function Director: vendor-funded hospitality from AED 301–1,000 per person.
- Chief Legal Officer plus relevant Executive Committee member: hospitality above AED 1,000 per person outside prohibited periods.
- Chief Legal Officer: senior executive conflicts.
- Compliance Officer plus Function Director: any single hospitality event above AED 300 per person, a third same-vendor event in 90 calendar days, or a rolling 90-day same-vendor total above AED 300.
- Chief Legal Officer plus relevant Executive Committee member: any further same-vendor hospitality after the employee's rolling 12-month total exceeds AED 1,000.
- Compliance Officer: company transfer, donation or documented disposal of a non-returnable gift above the personal-acceptance limit; personal retention cannot be approved.

## Required records

- Conflict declaration
- Event/organiser and vendor relationship
- Gift/hospitality value and source
- Business purpose and attendees
- Confirmation of no active prohibited decision
- Advance approval
- Decision and recusal arrangement
- Every accepted, declined or transferred offer
- Per-recipient market value
- Same-vendor rolling 90-day event count and value
- Same-vendor rolling 12-month value
- Gift return, company transfer, donation or disposal evidence

## Escalation rule

Escalate concealment, repeated offers, cash, travel, family ownership, quid-pro-quo language, or any offer connected to an active tender, selection, negotiation, dispute or renewal.

## Do not

- Do not divide a gift or hospitality cost among colleagues to reduce its apparent value.
- Do not accept hospitality during a prohibited decision period.
- Do not approve cash, personal travel, companion costs or personal entertainment as an exception.
- Do not obtain approval after attending.
- Do not split, alternate hosts or keep repeated sub-AED-300 hospitality off-register to avoid cumulative approval.
- Do not personally retain cash, cash equivalents or a gift above the personal-acceptance limit.
