# RET-003 — Proof of Purchase and Loyalty Transaction Lookup

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Retail Systems Manager
- **Audience:** Store associates, contact-centre agents
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.10
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

A customer has no paper receipt but provides a loyalty number or partial transaction details.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### RET-003-3.1

> A verified loyalty-account transaction, e-receipt, online order record or payment reference is acceptable proof of purchase.

### RET-003-3.2

> The associate must use only the minimum information needed to locate a transaction. For an account-linked loyalty transaction or e-receipt, disclosure is permitted only after the ownership factor and transaction factor in RET-003-3.9 both pass. A name, loyalty number, stated contact detail, store, item category or approximate amount is not verification.

### RET-003-3.3

> For a cash purchase with no loyalty record or receipt, the associate may search only within the approved POS lookup after the customer supplies all of these: store; purchase calendar date; a time window no wider than 30 minutes; exact total amount; and at least one exact item or SKU. The search must produce one unique match using all supplied fields. If a field is missing, more than one transaction matches, or any supplied field conflicts, no transaction details may be disclosed and RET-003-3.4 applies.

### RET-003-3.4

> If no unique transaction is found after approved searches, a standard refund cannot be processed. A claimed defect may still be accepted for inspection under RET-002, but no financial remedy may be promised until entitlement is established.

### RET-003-3.5

> A customer must never be asked to reveal a full card number, PIN or one-time password.

### RET-003-3.6

> A no-proof pattern is repeated only when the measurable count and time window in RET-003-3.8 are satisfied. The attempts may trigger OPS-004 review even though no refund was processed. A return supported by a verified digital transaction but no paper receipt is a verified return and is not a no-proof attempt.

### RET-003-3.7

> A Duty Manager may authorise additional permitted searches and record an inconclusive return attempt, but cannot approve a standard refund, credit note or exchange when no unique transaction or other controlling policy entitlement has been established. The phrase ‘evidence exception’ does not create authority to invent, assume or substitute a purchase record.

### RET-003-3.8

> For RET-003 and OPS-004, repeated unsuccessful no-proof activity means either the current attempt plus at least one earlier unsuccessful attempt involving the same verified customer or account and the same transaction or item within the preceding 30 calendar days, or the current attempt plus at least two earlier unsuccessful no-proof attempts involving the same verified customer or account within the preceding 90 calendar days. Only authorised case records may establish the count. Employees must not link anonymous people by appearance, name similarity or memory; an unlinked attempt remains a single attempt unless another independent OPS-004 trigger applies.

### RET-003-3.9

> Before an account-linked loyalty transaction or e-receipt is disclosed, two independent factors from different categories must pass: one ownership factor and one transaction factor. Approved ownership factors are a system-confirmed authenticated account session, completion of a verification link sent by the system to the contact channel already registered on the account, or original approved identification matched to the registered account name. Approved transaction factors are the exact order or receipt reference, or the combination of purchase calendar date, exact total amount and last four payment-card digits. A customer name, loyalty number, stated email address or phone number, store location, item category, approximate amount, or information displayed or suggested by the employee is not a factor. The employee must never receive or record a password, PIN or one-time code. A failed or unavailable factor cannot be replaced by extra non-qualifying details; the lookup stops and only the no-disclosure explanation may be given.

## Procedure

1. Ask for the loyalty number, order number, e-receipt or last four card digits plus date range.
2. Separate account-linked lookup from anonymous cash lookup. For an account-linked record, pass one ownership and one transaction factor from RET-003-3.9; for cash, apply the exact POS-match test in RET-003-3.3.
3. Use only the system result. Never ask the customer to read a password, PIN or one-time code to an employee, and do not reveal possible transaction details to help the customer answer.
4. For cash buyers without loyalty history, ask for the store, calendar date or narrow date range, approximate time, item and total, then search the authorised till archive.
5. Confirm two matching details and ensure the result is a unique transaction.
6. Before treating an attempt as repeated, apply the exact 30-day or 90-day count in RET-003-3.8 using authorised case records; do not link an anonymous customer by appearance or memory.
7. Open the transaction without exposing unrelated purchases and attach its reference to the case.
8. If nothing unique is found, do not process a standard refund; record the minimum permitted attempt details and apply OPS-004 if repeated attempts or other risk indicators are present.
9. Tell the customer: ‘I’m sorry, but I cannot confirm this purchase well enough to process a standard refund. If the item may be defective, I can still arrange an inspection, but I cannot promise a refund until we verify entitlement.’
10. Continue under the relevant return, inspection, warranty or complaint SOP.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: standard lookup, including the defined cash-purchase search.
- Duty Manager: additional permitted searches and recording of an inconclusive attempt only; no authority to approve a refund without a unique transaction or other established entitlement.
- Data Privacy Lead: suspected account misuse.

## Required records

- Search method
- Two matching verification points
- Transaction reference or failed unique-match result
- Minimum permitted attempt details
- Reason for access
- OPS-004 case reference where triggered
- Lookup route: account-linked or anonymous cash
- Factor categories and system pass/fail result—not secret values
- Unique cash-match fields and result
- Verified customer or account linkage where repeated
- Prior attempt dates and case references
- Repeated-trigger band used

## Escalation rule

Stop the lookup and escalate if the person cannot pass identity checks or disputes ownership of the loyalty account.

## Do not

- Do not reveal a customer's purchase history to another person.
- Do not write full card data in notes.
- Do not count a customer name, loyalty number, stated contact detail, store, item category or approximate amount as an account-verification factor.
- Do not disclose candidate transaction details, or receive or record a password, PIN or one-time code.
