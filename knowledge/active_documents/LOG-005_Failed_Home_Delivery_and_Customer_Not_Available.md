# LOG-005 — Failed Home Delivery and Customer Not Available

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Last-Mile Customer Experience Manager
- **Audience:** Drivers, dispatchers, contact centre and store teams
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.4
- **Effective:** 03 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

A delivery cannot be completed because the customer is absent, unreachable or the location is inaccessible.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-005-4.1

> The driver must make the approved contact attempts without disclosing order details to an unauthorised person.

### LOG-005-4.2

> Goods must not be left unattended unless the service and customer instruction explicitly permit safe-drop.

### LOG-005-4.3

> A failed-delivery reason, timestamp, location evidence and contact attempts must be recorded.

### LOG-005-4.4

> Redelivery fees or cancellation outcomes must follow the customer's service terms.

### LOG-005-4.5

> A redelivery fee waiver up to AED 100 requires Duty Manager approval under configured controls; AED 101–500 requires Customer Experience Manager approval; AED 501–50,000 requires Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 requires Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO.

### LOG-005-4.6

> The LOG-005 limit applies to waiver of the redelivery fee itself. It cannot be bypassed by recording the same waiver as complaint goodwill under RET-004. A genuinely separate goodwill gesture for distinct inconvenience follows RET-004, must be recorded separately, and is aggregated with the fee waiver and any other related customer value for FIN-001 authority.

## Procedure

1. Attempt contact using the delivery application.
2. Wait the approved ten-minute window in a safe location.
3. Record objective failure evidence.
4. Return goods to the authorised location.
5. Notify the customer and offer permitted redelivery/collection choices.
6. If compensation is requested, classify the redelivery-fee waiver separately from any genuinely distinct service-recovery gesture and obtain each applicable approval.

## Approval and decision authority

- Dispatcher: standard reattempt with no discretionary fee waiver.
- Duty Manager: fee waiver up to and including AED 100 under configured controls.
- Customer Experience Manager: AED 101–500 or disputed/repeated failure.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: AED 501–50,000.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.

## Required records

- Attempt timestamps
- Location evidence
- Reason code
- Return custody
- Redelivery-fee decision
- Separate goodwill decision if any
- Aggregate customer value
- Customer communication

## Escalation rule

Escalate aggression, unsafe access, temperature risk or suspected address/data error.

## Do not

- Do not leave goods with a neighbour without authorised instruction.
- Do not photograph people or private interiors.
- Do not relabel a redelivery-fee waiver as general goodwill to bypass LOG-005 authority.
