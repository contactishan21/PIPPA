## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-012-4.1

> Store teams must not refuse delivery of company-owned inventory due to missing Advance Shipping Notices (ASNs) or unreadable carton barcodes.

### OPS-012-4.2

> The receiver must initiate a "Blind Receipt" in the approved inventory system, mandating a 100% manual physical count of the carton's contents.

### OPS-012-4.3

> Duty Manager authority covers blind receipts up to AED 10,000 in estimated value; Regional Operations Manager approval is required above AED 10,000.

### OPS-012-4.4

> Missing ASN or unreadable barcode alone is not a reason to refuse safe, ordered company inventory; unordered, visibly damaged, unsafe, recalled or temperature-excursion goods must be segregated and handled under the applicable exception policy before acceptance.

### OPS-012-4.5

> Custody transfers only when the authorised receiver records physical handover by scan, signature or approved outage record. Bay location or unloading alone does not transfer custody.

### OPS-012-4.6

> A blind receipt requires two-person count, purchase-order validation, discrepancy record and later system reconciliation. Estimated value determines operational approval, while any financial adjustment follows FIN-001.
