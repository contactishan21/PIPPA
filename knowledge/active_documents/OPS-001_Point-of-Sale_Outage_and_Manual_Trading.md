# OPS-001 — Point-of-Sale Outage and Manual Trading

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

- **Status:** CURRENT

- **Effective:** 01 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

The POS, network or payment service is unavailable while customers are waiting.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-001-5.1

> Manual trading may begin only after the duty manager confirms the outage and opens an incident.

### OPS-001-5.2

> Card details must never be written, photographed or stored for later entry.

### OPS-001-5.3

> Cash-only continuity is permitted only where a functioning cash drawer, numbered manual receipts and safe capacity are available.

### OPS-001-5.4

> All manual sales must be reconciled and entered after service restoration under dual control.

## Procedure

1. Confirm scope and report the outage.
2. Display a clear customer notice.
3. Use approved offline payment mode if available; otherwise apply the authorised cash-only procedure.
4. Issue numbered manual receipts and secure copies.
5. Reconcile under two-person control when systems return.

## Approval and decision authority

- Duty manager activates continuity.
- Regional manager approves closure or trading beyond two hours.
- Technology incident lead owns restoration.

## Required records

- Incident number
- Manual receipt sequence
- Cash totals
- Reconciliation sign-off

## Escalation rule

Close trading if customer safety, transaction integrity or secure cash capacity cannot be maintained.

## Do not

- Do not write down card details.
- Do not use personal payment apps.
- Do not enter reconstructed sales without a second checker.
