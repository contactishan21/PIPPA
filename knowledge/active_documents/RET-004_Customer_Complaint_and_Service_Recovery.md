# RET-004 — Customer Complaint and Service Recovery

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## When to use this document

A customer is dissatisfied, asks for compensation or wants to escalate.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### RET-004-4.1

> Employees must acknowledge the concern, summarise it back to the customer and separate what can be resolved immediately from what requires investigation.

### RET-004-4.2

> Service recovery gestures must be proportionate and must not be described as an admission of legal liability.

### RET-004-4.3

> A complaint involving safety, discrimination, privacy, violence, media contact or threatened legal action must be escalated immediately.

### RET-004-4.4

> The customer must receive a case reference and a realistic next-update time.

### RET-004-4.5

> Financial service-recovery authority is: Duty Manager up to AED 250 under configured controls; Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager for AED 251–10,000; Customer Experience Director plus Finance Manager for AED 10,001–50,000; Chief Customer Officer plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### RET-004-4.6

> A specific operational fee waiver, refund or price correction must follow its controlling functional policy and must not be relabelled as general goodwill to use a higher local limit. A separate goodwill gesture may be considered only for a distinct customer impact, must be recorded separately, and must be aggregated with all related customer value when applying FIN-001.

### RET-004-4.7

> A complaint involving the return, surrender, replacement or refund of physical goods remains a return for control purposes. If the gross goods value exceeds AED 5,000, proof is absent, fraud indicators exist or OPS-004 otherwise applies, the Duty Manager hold and Loss Prevention review must be completed before any refund, credit note, exchange or disposal. A separate goodwill gesture for distinct service failure may proceed only within RET-004 authority and must not release or extinguish the controlled goods claim.

## Procedure

1. Listen without interrupting and acknowledge the impact.
2. Confirm the facts and the outcome the customer is seeking.
3. Identify whether physical goods are being returned, surrendered, replaced or refunded; if so, apply RET-004-4.7 and OPS-004 before selecting a complaint remedy.
4. Classify any requested amount as a specific fee waiver, refund, price correction or genuinely separate goodwill gesture before selecting an approval route.
5. Resolve within authority or create a complaint case.
6. Explain the next step and timing in simple terms.
7. Record the outcome and close only after the customer is informed.

## Approval and decision authority

- Associate: apology and operational correction with no financial gesture.
- Duty Manager: goodwill up to and including AED 250 under configured service-recovery controls.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: AED 251–10,000.
- Customer Experience Director plus Finance Manager: AED 10,001–50,000.
- Chief Customer Officer plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.

## Required records

- Complaint summary
- Customer-requested outcome
- Benefit category
- Related fee waiver or refund
- Aggregate customer value
- Evidence
- Decision and approver
- Communication log

## Escalation rule

Immediate escalation categories are safety, privacy, discrimination, violence, regulator/media contact and threatened litigation.

## Do not

- Do not argue about the customer's feelings.
- Do not guarantee an outcome before facts are checked.
- Do not relabel a fee waiver or refund as goodwill to obtain a higher local limit.
- Do not offer personal reimbursement.
