# QUA-001 — Corporate Product Recall and Withdrawal

- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

We just received a notice that a product is unsafe—what is the immediate action?

## Authoritative policy clauses

### QUA-001-4.1

> A "Stop Sale" alert issued by the Quality Assurance Director mandates the immediate physical removal of the specified SKU from all store shelves, stockrooms, and e-commerce platforms within 2 hours.

### QUA-001-4.2

> Under no circumstances may an employee sell, discount, or gift a recalled item.

### QUA-001-4.3

> Recalled inventory must be quarantined in a clearly marked 'DO NOT TOUCH' zone pending RTV (Return to Vendor) or secure destruction instructions from Quality Assurance.

### QUA-001-4.4

> The recall notice must identify SKU, lot or batch, locations, effective time, customer remedy, disposition route and accountable owner. Stores record quantities removed and zero-sale verification.

### QUA-001-4.5

> E-commerce and store systems must be blocked within the same two-hour window. Known affected customers are notified only through the approved Quality, Legal and Communications plan.

### QUA-001-4.6

> Destruction or RTV follows LOG-013 and FIN-001 authority; evidence includes witness record, quantity, method, carrier or supplier credit and final reconciliation.
