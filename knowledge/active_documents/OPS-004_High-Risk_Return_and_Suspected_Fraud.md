# OPS-004 — High-Risk Return and Suspected Fraud

## When to use this document

A return shows unusual patterns, altered proof, high value or suspected abuse.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-004-5.1

> Employees must remain neutral and must not accuse a customer of fraud.

### OPS-004-5.2

> Returns above AED 5,000, a repeated-attempt pattern established under RET-003-3.8, serial-number mismatch or altered proof require a Duty Manager hold and Loss Prevention review. A repeated-attempt trigger does not mean an earlier refund succeeded and does not authorise payment without proof under RET-003.

### OPS-004-5.3

> Only authorised loss-prevention staff may access cross-store pattern data.

### OPS-004-5.4

> Customer safety takes priority; employees must disengage if behaviour becomes threatening.

### OPS-004-5.5

> For high-risk returns, the Loss Prevention Manager plus Regional Operations Manager authorise remedies from AED 5,001–10,000; the Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller authorise AED 10,001–250,000; and the CFO plus CEO authorise amounts above AED 250,000.

### OPS-004-5.6

> Classification follows the underlying transaction, not the case label. A physical return with gross goods value above AED 5,000, missing proof or another risk trigger remains subject to OPS-004 even if it is opened as a complaint, service recovery, goodwill request, exchange or administrative cancellation. RET-004 cannot be used to bypass the hold, Loss Prevention review or high-risk remedy authority.

### OPS-004-5.7

> The repeated-attempt trigger uses the measurable counts and periods in RET-003-3.8. A system flag based on authorised case records may instruct the Duty Manager to hold the current attempt; only Loss Prevention may review cross-store pattern details. If the customer or account cannot be reliably linked, staff must not create a match from appearance, similar names or memory, and the current attempt is not treated as repeated unless another independent trigger in OPS-004-5.2 applies.

## Procedure

1. Identify the underlying goods transaction and place it on hold when OPS-004 applies, regardless of whether the case arrived through returns, complaints, goodwill, exchange or cancellation.
2. Verify transaction and product identifiers.
3. Where proof cannot be established, do not process a refund; record the minimum permitted attempt details and check authorised pattern indicators.
4. Explain that an additional review is needed without alleging wrongdoing.
5. Retain only items the customer voluntarily submits under an issued receipt.
6. Open a case and contact Loss Prevention when a value, evidence, serial or altered-proof trigger applies, or when authorised records satisfy the exact repeated-attempt count in RET-003-3.8.
7. Communicate the approved outcome and document it.

## Approval and decision authority

- Duty Manager: place the transaction on review; no financial remedy above AED 5,000.
- Loss Prevention Manager plus Regional Operations Manager: evidence decision and customer remedy from AED 5,001–10,000.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller: customer remedy from AED 10,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: customer remedy above AED 250,000, in line with FIN-001.

## Required records

- Transaction evidence or failed lookup result
- Minimum permitted attempt details
- Product serial
- Observed facts only
- Case number
- Decision
- Repeated-attempt count and time window
- Authorised prior-case references
- Linkage basis

## Escalation rule

Call security or emergency services for threats, violence or immediate danger.

## Do not

- Do not use words such as thief or fraudster.
- Do not physically block a customer.
- Do not search personal belongings.
- Do not create a repeated-customer match from appearance, a similar name or employee memory.
