## Authoritative policy clauses

### HR-003-4.1

> Frontline retail employees are issued three standard uniform sets annually at company expense.

### HR-003-4.2

> Replacement of lost or negligently damaged uniforms will result in a documented recovery review at verified cost value.

### HR-003-4.3

> Store Manager authority covers standard replacements up to AED 500.

### HR-003-4.4

> Ordinary wear, fit change, safety-related replacement and company-caused damage are company costs and are not employee negligence.

### HR-003-4.5

> No payroll deduction may be made automatically. HR must confirm the factual basis, obtain any consent required by law and apply PAY-001; disputed liability is held pending HR review.

### HR-003-4.6

> Replacement cost above AED 500 requires Regional Operations Manager plus HR Manager approval and the applicable FIN-001 approval.
