## When to use this document

## Authoritative policy clauses

### FAC-001-4.1

> Emergency repair authority: Facilities Manager up to AED 10,000; Head of Operations plus Finance Manager up to AED 50,000; VP/COO plus Finance Controller for AED 50,001–250,000.

### FAC-001-4.2

> The Duty Manager may immediately isolate the area, stop trading and contact emergency services or the approved emergency contractor where delay threatens people or property.

### FAC-001-4.3

> Contractor identity, insurance, access, permit-to-work and electrical or structural competence must be verified before non-life-saving work begins.

### FAC-001-4.4

> Emergency repair spend follows FIN-001 for all bands. Verbal approval must include all required approvers, be recorded within one business day under PRC-007, and any missed deadline follows PRC-004.

4. Obtain each required functional and financial approval before commitment, release, payment, posting or disposal.
5. Record the decision, approvers, evidence, system references and closure outcome, then complete any required reconciliation or follow-up.

## Approval and decision authority

- The operational authority stated in the clauses governs the functional decision.
- Any spend, refund, credit, loss, write-off, settlement or commitment also requires the applicable FIN-001 financial approval; the stricter cumulative combination applies.
- No approver may self-approve, split related transactions or treat investigation, validation or proof acceptance as financial approval.
