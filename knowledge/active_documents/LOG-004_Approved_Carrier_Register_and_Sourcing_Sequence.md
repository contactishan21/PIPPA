## When to use this document

A team needs to know which external fleet suppliers may be contacted first.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### LOG-004-4.1

> Only carriers shown as Active in the controlled register may be mobilised without a CPO exception.

### LOG-004-4.2

> For UAE last-mile vans, the sourcing sequence is: Sandline Mobility FZ-LLC, Gulf Arc Transport LLC, then Cedar Route Logistics LLC, subject to lane and capacity.

### LOG-004-4.3

> A carrier's approved status applies only to the listed service category, geography and vehicle type.

### LOG-004-4.4

> The register owner must confirm insurance and contract expiry before award.

### LOG-004-4.5

> Approved-carrier selection or a CPO non-approved-carrier exception is a sourcing decision only. It does not approve transport spend, which must separately follow LOG-002 and FIN-001.

## Procedure

1. Open the controlled register and filter Active carriers.
2. Match category, geography and vehicle type.
3. Contact vendors in ranked order and record capacity/rate responses.
4. If none can serve, raise an exception under PRC-007/PRC-009.
5. Recheck contract and insurance before activation.

## Approval and decision authority

- Transport Procurement Lead: standard approved-carrier selection; this is a sourcing decision, not spend approval.
- CPO: non-approved carrier sourcing exception; FIN-001 spend approval remains mandatory.
- Risk/Legal: expired or changed controls.

## Required records

- Register snapshot
- Contact sequence
- Quotes/capacity
- Expiry validation
- Exception

## Escalation rule

Escalate if all approved carriers decline, use unapproved subcontractors or cannot meet the required vehicle control.

## Do not

- Do not assume an approved courier is approved for heavy vehicles.
- Do not select solely on a verbal rate.
