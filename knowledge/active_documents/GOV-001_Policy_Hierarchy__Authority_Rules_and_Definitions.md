# GOV-001 — Policy Hierarchy, Authority Rules and Definitions

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## When to use this document

Use this document whenever two sources appear inconsistent, a threshold boundary is unclear, or a role, time period, manual transaction or emergency approval needs interpretation.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### GOV-001-2.1

> The order of precedence is: applicable law and immediate life-safety direction; this governance policy and FIN-001; current function policy; current SOP or work instruction; training or guidance. A local document may add a stricter control but may not weaken a higher-level control.

### GOV-001-2.2

> If current sources remain inconsistent, the employee must apply the stricter safe control, pause any irreversible action and escalate to both document owners. PIPPA must state the conflict and must not invent a resolution.

### GOV-001-2.3

> Financial thresholds use total aggregated exposure. ‘Up to’ and ‘up to and including’ include the stated amount; a range beginning at the next dirham excludes the lower band. Splitting by invoice, day, item, route, store or supplier is prohibited.

### GOV-001-2.4

> FIN-001 is the financial authority floor for commitments, refunds, credits, write-offs, waivers, claims and settlements. A function policy may require an additional operational approver or a lower local limit, but cannot remove FIN-001 approval.

### GOV-001-2.5

> Operational approval confirms the business, customer, safety or quality decision. Financial approval authorises the monetary effect. Where both are listed, both are mandatory and neither substitutes for the other.

### GOV-001-2.6

> A recorded verbal emergency approval is permitted only when the normal approval system is unavailable. It authorises the minimum commitment at the correct value band and must be documented within one business day; it never replaces a written contract, call-off, insurance, safety, privacy or custody control required before mobilisation.

### GOV-001-2.7

> A calendar day is every day including weekends and public holidays. A business day is a normal working day for the responsible function in the UAE, excluding its recognised weekend and public holidays. If a document says only ‘day’, it means calendar day.

### GOV-001-2.8

> A system-issued credit note or refund is completed end-to-end in the authorised POS or finance workflow. A manual transaction includes an off-system ledger entry, manual bank instruction, spreadsheet upload, duplicate re-entry or other intervention outside that controlled workflow.

### GOV-001-2.9

> The Regional Operations Manager owns store execution and operational approval. The Regional Customer Resolution Manager owns disputed customer-resolution findings. The Regional Operations Director is the higher operational authority. These are distinct roles.

### GOV-001-2.10

> Approval of a sourcing route, such as a sole-source exemption, is not approval of spend. Contract signature, financial approval, risk approval and operational readiness remain separate mandatory controls.

### GOV-001-2.11

> Roles from different functions are not assumed to be equivalent or ranked by title. Where a transaction crosses functions, every role named in an explicit joint-control matrix is mandatory and approvals are cumulative. A policy must not instruct employees to choose a ‘stricter’ role by comparing titles. If no explicit joint matrix covers the situation, pause the irreversible action and escalate to every affected document owner and the Policy Governance Council.

### GOV-001-2.12

> Unless a function policy states a different measurable rule, repeated means the current event plus at least one prior linked event within the preceding 30 calendar days, or the current event plus at least two prior linked events within the preceding 90 calendar days. The control subject must be established through authorised records, such as the same verified person or account, vendor, SKU or batch, till or custodian, vehicle or driver, route or process owner. Staff must not create links from appearance, name similarity or memory. A safety, fraud, legal or regulatory concern may still be escalated on the first event; it must be recorded under that independent trigger, not labelled repeated.

### GOV-001-2.13

> If no CURRENT, retrieval-eligible policy directly answers the employee's question with sufficient evidence, PIPPA must state: ‘I cannot find a current policy that answers this question. Please contact your line manager or the relevant process owner.’ PIPPA must not use a partially related policy as a substitute or infer a process that is not documented.

### GOV-001-2.14

> PIPPA may provide suggested wording only when it is useful to the employee's request and the intended recipient is clear from the question or controlling policy. It must not assume that the recipient is a customer, vendor, manager or colleague.

## Procedure

1. Identify every current source that applies to the decision.
2. First test whether a CURRENT, retrieval-eligible policy directly answers the question; if not, use the exact no-match response in GOV-001-2.13.
3. Apply the precedence order and use the stricter non-conflicting requirement.
4. Where a rule uses repeated without its own threshold, identify the control subject and apply the default 30-day or 90-day count in GOV-001-2.12 using authorised records.
5. Aggregate the full financial exposure and identify operational plus financial approvers.
6. If any conflict, undefined boundary or missing authority remains, pause irreversible action and escalate to the document owners and Policy Governance Council.
7. Record the interpretation or approved exception and update the affected source; do not leave a recurring decision in email only.

## Approval and decision authority

- Up to AED 10,000: Manager plus independent budget/finance check.
- AED 10,001–50,000: Director plus Finance Manager.
- AED 50,001–250,000: VP/COO plus Finance Controller.
- Above AED 250,000: CFO plus CEO.
- Function-specific documents may add approvers or lower caps but may not relax these bands.

## Required records

- Sources consulted
- Aggregated value/exposure
- Operational and financial approvals
- Conflict or exception decision
- Policy-owner remediation
- Repeated-event subject and linkage
- Prior event dates and references
- Applied 30-day or 90-day band

## Escalation rule

Escalate unresolved conflicts, absent approvers, undefined financial authority, pressure to bypass a control, or any proposed interpretation that would weaken FIN-001.

## Do not

- Do not choose the easier of two conflicting rules.
- Do not assume a similar job title is the same role.
- Do not treat route approval, budget availability or verbal consent as complete transaction authority.
