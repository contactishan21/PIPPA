## Authoritative policy clauses

### HR-006-4.1

> Education allowance is restricted to eligible management tiers and covers a maximum of three legal dependents up to age 18.

### HR-006-4.2

> Reimbursements are capped at AED 40,000 per child, per academic year, payable directly upon submission of official school invoices.

### HR-006-4.3

> HR Director plus Finance Director must approve all education allowance payouts.

### HR-006-4.4

> The AED 40,000 per child per academic year limit and three-dependent limit are hard policy caps. The CFO cannot waive them; a different benefit requires an approved amendment to the Benefits Schedule.

### HR-006-4.5

> Eligibility, dependency, age, academic year, currency conversion and duplicate reimbursement must be verified before payment.

### HR-006-4.6

> Payment requires an official school invoice and proof of enrolment; any employee reimbursement also follows FIN-002.
