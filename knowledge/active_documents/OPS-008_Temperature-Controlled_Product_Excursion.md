# OPS-008 — Temperature-Controlled Product Excursion

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Food Safety & Quality Director
- **Audience:** Store teams, warehouse teams and duty managers
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.8
- **Effective:** 04 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

Chilled or frozen stock is outside its approved temperature range.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### OPS-008-5.1

> Affected stock must be isolated immediately and marked ‘HOLD—DO NOT SELL’ until disposition is approved.

### OPS-008-5.2

> Time, measured temperature, product, quantity and equipment condition must be recorded.

### OPS-008-5.3

> Employees must not return held stock to sale based on appearance or touch.

### OPS-008-5.4

> Only Quality may approve release; the Duty Manager may approve disposal up to AED 2,000 after Quality instruction and only after store custody has begun under OPS-008-5.6.

### OPS-008-5.5

> After Quality disposition, store cold-chain disposal up to AED 2,000 may be approved by a Duty Manager under configured inventory controls; AED 2,001–50,000 requires Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000 requires Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller; and above AED 250,000 requires CFO plus CEO.

### OPS-008-5.6

> Store custody begins only when an authorised store receiver has both taken physical control of the affected units and completed the approved receiving scan or signed handover. Arrival at the receiving bay, opening vehicle doors or starting unloading does not by itself transfer custody.

### OPS-008-5.7

> If an excursion is discovered during unloading and custody evidence is missing, disputed or inconsistent, stop the handover and have Quality define the affected batch. The same cumulative joint-control matrix applies in OPS-008 and LOG-007: up to AED 2,000—Store Duty Manager, Transport or Warehouse Manager, and Finance Manager; AED 2,001–5,000—Regional Operations Manager, Transport or Warehouse Manager, and Finance Manager; AED 5,001–25,000—Regional Operations Manager, Logistics Director, and Finance Manager; AED 25,001–50,000—Regional Operations Manager, Supply Chain Director, and Finance Manager; AED 50,001–250,000—Regional Operations Director, COO, and Finance Controller; above AED 250,000—Regional Operations Director, Supply Chain Director, CFO, and CEO. Quality disposition is mandatory in every band. All named roles are cumulative; none is selected by comparing job titles.

## Procedure

1. Stop sale or handover and isolate affected stock.
2. Take a calibrated reading and record time.
3. Record the physical-control point and receiving scan or signed-handover status for the affected units.
4. Check equipment alarm and excursion start estimate.
5. Contact Quality with product and traceability details.
6. Apply LOG-007 before completed custody transfer and OPS-008 after completed custody transfer; when evidence is unclear, use the explicit cumulative matrix in OPS-008-5.7 and LOG-007-5.7.
7. Release, transfer or dispose only on written instruction.

## Approval and decision authority

- Quality Specialist: product disposition; this does not itself approve a write-off.
- Duty Manager under configured inventory control: disposal up to and including AED 2,000 after Quality instruction.
- Regional Operations Manager plus Finance Manager: AED 2,001–50,000.
- Regional Operations Director plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.

## Required records

- Temperature log
- SKU/batch
- Quantity/value
- Photos
- Quality disposition
- Receiving scan or signed handover
- Custody owner at detection
- Joint approval where disputed

## Escalation rule

Escalate potential customer exposure, widespread equipment failure or recalled product immediately.

## Do not

- Do not taste or sell held stock.
- Do not reset an alarm before recording it.
- Do not discard traceability labels.
