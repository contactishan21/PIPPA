## Authoritative policy clauses

### MKT-001-4.1

> Authority to approve marketing agency commitments and influencer gifting campaigns is based on the total expected campaign value: Head of Marketing up to AED 50,000; Head of Marketing plus Chief Commercial Officer for AED 50,001–250,000; and CFO plus CEO above AED 250,000.

### MKT-001-4.2

> Agency committed spend is valued at the total contract or campaign commitment, including extensions and related statements of work. Influencer gifting is valued at retail value per recipient and in aggregate across a related campaign.

### MKT-001-4.3

> Agency spend follows FIN-001 in addition to marketing approval. Gifting also requires PRC-005 ethics review when a recipient is connected to a tender, public official, customer decision-maker or other conflict risk.

### MKT-001-4.4

> The register must capture recipient, relationship, retail value, aggregate campaign value, disclosure obligation, approval and proof of delivery; related gifts may not be split.

- No approver may self-approve, split related transactions or treat investigation, validation or proof acceptance as financial approval.
