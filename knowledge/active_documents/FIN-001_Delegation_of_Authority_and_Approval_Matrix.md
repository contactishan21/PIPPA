# FIN-001 — Delegation of Authority and Approval Matrix

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## Control information

- **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
- **Audience:** All employees who request, approve, commit or sign
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Effective:** 03 September 2026
- **Review due:** 01 March 2027

## When to use this document

An employee needs to know who can approve spending or sign a commitment.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### FIN-001-3.1

> Approval authority applies to the full expected commitment and cannot be delegated informally.

### FIN-001-3.2

> Budget availability does not itself grant authority to commit or sign.

### FIN-001-3.3

> A person must not approve their own expense, vendor selection or transaction from which they benefit.

### FIN-001-3.4

> Electronic approval must come from the named approver's authenticated account and remain in the system audit trail.

### FIN-001-3.5

> This master matrix applies to commitments, refunds, credit notes, write-offs, fee waivers, vendor service credits, claims and financial settlements. A function policy may impose a lower limit or an additional approver, but it may not remove or raise the financial authority required here.

### FIN-001-3.6

> Where a function decision and a financial decision are both required, both approvals must be obtained; operational ownership does not substitute for financial authority.

### FIN-001-3.7

> For routine high-volume customer transactions up to an expressly stated local limit, configured system controls plus independent post-transaction Finance reconciliation may satisfy the independent financial check. Any off-system/manual execution or amount above the local limit requires the named Finance approver before execution.

## Procedure

1. Calculate total commitment including options and taxes.
2. Identify the transaction type and required specialist approvals.
3. Route to the correct financial approver.
4. Obtain procurement/legal review and authorised signature where needed.
5. Retain the approval with the transaction.

## Approval and decision authority

- Manager plus independent budget/finance check: up to and including AED 10,000.
- Director plus Finance Manager: AED 10,001–50,000.
- VP/COO plus Finance Controller: AED 50,001–250,000.
- CFO plus CEO: above AED 250,000.

## Required records

- Value calculation
- Budget confirmation
- Approval record
- Specialist approvals
- Signed commitment

## Escalation rule

Escalate uncertainty about aggregation, acting appointments, conflicts or an absent approver; do not substitute a lower approver.

## Do not

- Do not split transactions.
- Do not approve your own benefit.
- Do not treat chat consent as signature authority unless captured in the approved workflow.
