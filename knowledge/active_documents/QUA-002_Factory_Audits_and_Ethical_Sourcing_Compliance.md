## Authoritative policy clauses

### QUA-002-4.1

> All private-label manufacturers must undergo and pass a certified third-party ethical sourcing and social compliance audit prior to onboarding and initial purchase order generation.

### QUA-002-4.2

> Temporary waivers or exceptions for audit compliance are strictly prohibited.

### QUA-002-4.3

> Factories scoring below 70% on the annual quality/compliance index must be placed on a 90-day remediation plan or terminated.

### QUA-002-4.4

> Quality Assurance owns auditor approval and the pass decision. A score of 70% or above passes only if no critical finding exists; any critical forced-labour, child-labour or imminent-safety finding fails regardless of score.

### QUA-002-4.5

> A score below 70% requires a documented 90-day remediation plan only where Legal and Quality determine remediation is permissible; otherwise onboarding or supply is terminated. Re-audit is required before release from remediation.

### QUA-002-4.6

> The control applies to relevant subcontractors and production sites, and purchasing may not shift work to an unaudited site.

## Procedure

1. Confirm that the request falls within this policy's scope and identify the transaction, employee, vendor, asset or incident involved.
2. Collect the source records and calculate the complete value, time period and related transactions before choosing an approval band.
3. Apply the controlling clauses and all referenced policies; pause the process where safety, identity, custody, legality or evidence is unresolved.
4. Obtain each required functional and financial approval before commitment, release, payment, posting or disposal.
5. Record the decision, approvers, evidence, system references and closure outcome, then complete any required reconciliation or follow-up.

## Approval and decision authority

- The operational authority stated in the clauses governs the functional decision.
- Any spend, refund, credit, loss, write-off, settlement or commitment also requires the applicable FIN-001 financial approval; the stricter cumulative combination applies.
- No approver may self-approve, split related transactions or treat investigation, validation or proof acceptance as financial approval.

## Escalation rule

Escalate to the Legal Director if an audit reveals child labor or forced labor violations.
