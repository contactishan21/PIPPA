# PRC-003 — Vendor Legal Entity, Ownership or Bank Change

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — This document, company, people, limits, vendors and procedures are fictional. It is designed only to test the PIPPA knowledge-assistant prototype and must not be used as legal, financial, safety or operational advice.**

## When to use this document

A vendor changes from sole establishment to LLC, changes ownership/name, or requests new bank details.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### PRC-003-4.1

> A legal-entity change is not treated as a simple profile edit; Procurement must determine whether a new vendor record and contract novation or replacement are required.

### PRC-003-4.2

> Payments must be paused when the invoicing entity or bank beneficiary does not match the approved vendor master.

### PRC-003-4.3

> New bank details require independent callback verification and maker-checker approval.

### PRC-003-4.4

> The business owner must not instruct Accounts Payable to bypass validation because services have already been delivered.

### PRC-003-4.5

> A CPO transition exception may approve sequencing or temporary suspension arrangements only. It cannot waive confirmation of the legal contracting entity, sanctions screening, independent bank verification, maker-checker vendor-master approval, contract novation or the separate payment approvals in FIN-001 and FIN-003.

## Procedure

1. Collect official registration, ownership, tax and bank-change documents.
2. Pause affected invoices without rejecting them.
3. Procurement and Legal assess whether the contracting party has changed.
4. Complete due diligence and independent bank verification.
5. If transition sequencing cannot follow the normal order, document the narrow CPO exception under PRC-003-4.5 without waiving any listed control.
6. Create/modify the vendor master under dual control.
7. Execute novation or a new contract where required, then release validated invoices.

## Approval and decision authority

- Vendor governance manager: profile change.
- Legal counsel: entity/contract determination.
- Finance master-data approver: bank or vendor-master change.
- CPO: sequencing or temporary-suspension exception only; no authority to waive legal-entity, screening, bank, maker-checker, contract or payment controls.

## Required records

- Official change documents
- Due-diligence result
- Callback record
- Legal decision
- Maker-checker audit trail

## Escalation rule

Escalate urgent payment pressure, domain changes, mismatched beneficiary names or instructions to keep the change confidential.

## Do not

- Do not overwrite the old entity without an audit trail.
- Do not verify bank data using a phone number from the change email.
- Do not pay a mismatched beneficiary.
