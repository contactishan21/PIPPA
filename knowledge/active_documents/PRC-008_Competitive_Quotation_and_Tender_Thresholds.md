## When to use this document

A team needs to know how many quotes or what sourcing route applies to a purchase.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### PRC-008-4.1

> Total expected contract value, including extensions and options, determines the sourcing threshold.

### PRC-008-4.2

> Up to AED 10,000 requires one documented market check; AED 10,001–50,000 requires three written quotations where available.

### PRC-008-4.3

> AED 50,001–250,000 requires a Procurement-led request for quotation; above AED 250,000 requires a formal tender unless an approved exemption applies.

### PRC-008-4.4

> Purchases must not be split or shortened to avoid a threshold.

### PRC-008-4.5

> A sourcing-route approval or sole-source exception does not approve spend. The resulting commitment must separately follow every financial band in FIN-001, including CFO plus CEO approval above AED 250,000.

### PRC-008-4.6

> For an aggregated purchase up to AED 10,000, one documented market check may support a direct purchase from one vendor without a sole-source exemption. The record must demonstrate price and route reasonableness using an approved catalogue or framework, a recent comparable purchase, a published price, or a documented check of an alternative supplier. A vendor quotation by itself is not a market check.

### PRC-008-4.7

> A sole-source exception is required when the requestor specifies one vendor and no independent market evidence is available, or when only one supplier can meet a documented compatibility, exclusivity, emergency or technical requirement. Exception authority is Procurement Manager up to AED 10,000; Procurement Director for AED 10,001-50,000; and CPO above AED 50,000. Above AED 250,000, Tender Committee review is also required. These approvals govern the sourcing route only; FIN-001 financial authority remains separate.

### PRC-008-4.8

> When three written quotations are required but fewer qualified suppliers are available after documented, good-faith invitations, Procurement must retain the supplier search, invitations, responses or non-responses, conflict check and price-reasonableness evidence. Two valid quotations require Procurement Director approval; one valid quotation also becomes a sole-source exception under PRC-008-4.7. For an RFQ or formal tender, any reduction in the approved competition route requires CPO plus Tender Committee approval before award. Limited competition never approves spend or permits criteria to be changed after bids are known.

## Procedure

1. Estimate and aggregate total value across the full term.
2. Check approved catalogues/frameworks first.
3. For purchases up to AED 10,000, document an independent price or alternative-supplier check; do not rely only on the chosen vendor's quotation.
4. If independent market evidence is unavailable or the need is genuinely unique, document the sole-source reason and obtain the value-based sourcing exception.
5. For higher thresholds, invite the required quotations or run the RFQ or tender route.
6. If fewer qualified bids are received, apply PRC-008-4.8 before award; do not silently treat ‘where available’ as discretion.
7. Document objective criteria before receiving bids.
8. Evaluate, approve and retain the sourcing record.

## Approval and decision authority

- Budget Owner: business need.
- Procurement or Buying Manager: standard documented market check and sole-source exception up to AED 10,000.
- Procurement Director: sole-source exception from AED 10,001-50,000.
- CPO: sole-source exception above AED 50,000; this is not financial approval and cannot override FIN-001.
- Tender Committee: formal-tender governance and review of sole-source exceptions above AED 250,000.
- Financial approvers under FIN-001: separate commitment approval.

## Required records

- Aggregated value calculation
- Market-check evidence
- Quotes/bids
- Sole-source rationale and approval where applicable
- Criteria and scoring
- Conflict declarations
- Award approval

## Escalation rule

Escalate vendor scarcity, sole-source claims, shared bidder details or suspected collusion.

## Do not

- Do not split demand.
- Do not treat the selected vendor's own quotation as independent market evidence.
- Do not reveal one bidder's price to another.
- Do not define criteria after bids arrive.
