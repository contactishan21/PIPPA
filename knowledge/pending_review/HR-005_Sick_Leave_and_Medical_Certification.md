## Authoritative policy clauses

### HR-005-4.1

> Absences due to illness exceeding two consecutive calendar days require a certified medical report from a licensed practitioner.

### HR-005-4.2

> Sick leave compensation is strictly governed by UAE statutory limits.

### HR-005-4.3

> Employees must notify their manager through the approved channel as soon as reasonably possible on each absence. A medical report is not required solely because an absence is one or two consecutive calendar days unless lawfully requested by HR for a documented reason.

### HR-005-4.4

> HR must apply the current controlled statutory sick-leave schedule and effective legal source; this policy does not freeze or paraphrase statutory pay entitlements.

### HR-005-4.5

> Medical information is confidential, access-restricted and retained under the approved HR retention schedule.

### HR-005-4.6

> Employees should inform their line manager of a sickness absence as early as possible and, where reasonably practicable, before the start of their next scheduled shift.

### HR-005-4.7

> For an absence requiring a medical report under HR-005-4.1, an employee based in the UAE should provide a government-approved medical sick-leave certificate issued by a clinic or hospital within the UAE.

### HR-005-4.8

> Subject to HR-005-4.4 and the applicable controlled legal source, the current company sick-leave schedule provides up to 15 days of fully paid sick leave and a further 30 days of partially paid sick leave in each year. Sick leave beyond those 45 days is unpaid.

### HR-005-4.9

> An employee may notify their line manager of a sickness absence by telephone call, message, or email. Each of these is an approved notification channel under HR-005-4.3 and HR-005-4.6.

### HR-005-4.10

> Partially paid sick leave under HR-005-4.8 is paid at 50% of the employee's applicable pay.

## Procedure

1. Notify the line manager of a sickness absence as early as possible and, where reasonably practicable, before the start of the next scheduled shift, by telephone call, message, or email.
2. For an absence exceeding two consecutive calendar days, provide the certified medical report required by HR-005-4.1. UAE-based employees should use the certificate described in HR-005-4.7.
3. HR applies the current controlled statutory sick-leave schedule and effective legal source, including HR-005-4.8 where it remains applicable.
4. Apply the paid and unpaid sick-leave bands in HR-005-4.8. Partially paid sick leave is paid at 50% under HR-005-4.10.
5. Restrict medical information to authorised access and retain it under the approved HR retention schedule.
6. Escalate more than 15 uncertified sick days in a rolling 12-month period to HR.
