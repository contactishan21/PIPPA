# PRC-002 — Late and Retrospective Vendor Invoices

## Control information

- **Owner:** Accounts Payable Director
- **Audience:** Requestors, vendor managers, procurement and accounts payable
- **Status:** CURRENT
- **Version:** 1.11-candidate
- **Effective:** 05 September 2026

## When to use this document

A vendor forgot to submit an invoice for work completed five months ago and now has complete supporting details.

## Authoritative policy clauses

### PRC-002-5.1

> An invoice whose valid-submission age under PRC-002-5.8 is more than 90 calendar days after service completion is late and requires a Late Invoice Declaration from the business owner.

### PRC-002-5.2

> A valid invoice first submitted after 90 and up to and including 180 calendar days may be processed when the service is evidenced, the charge is valid, budget remains available and tax requirements are met.

### PRC-002-5.3

> A valid invoice first submitted more than 180 calendar days after service completion requires Finance Controller approval in addition to the Budget Owner and Accounts Payable. Internal processing time after the fixed valid-submission timestamp does not change this band.

### PRC-002-5.4

> Payment must not be promised until duplicate-payment, vendor status, purchase-order and receipt checks are complete.

### PRC-002-5.5

> Approval to accept a late invoice addresses lateness only. It does not replace the original purchase, budget, receipt, exception or payment approvals required by PRC-004, FIN-001 and FIN-003.

### PRC-002-5.6

> If the original vendor invoice is missing or irreproducible, Accounts Payable must first request a supplier-issued duplicate or replacement bearing the original supply details and a clear duplicate designation. If the supplier cannot issue one, payment may proceed only where Legal and Tax confirm that a controlled invoice substitute is permissible, the vendor identity and amount are independently evidenced by the contract or PO, receipt or service-completion record, supplier correspondence or account statement where obtainable, and a full duplicate-payment search. The substitute is not a tax invoice and recoverable VAT must not be claimed without legally valid tax evidence.

### PRC-002-5.7

> A controlled invoice-substitute exception is additional to the normal purchase, receipt and payment approvals. Up to AED 10,000 requires Accounts Payable Manager, Procurement Manager and Finance Manager; AED 10,001–50,000 requires Accounts Payable Director, Procurement Director and Finance Director; AED 50,001–250,000 requires Finance Controller, Procurement Director and Internal Audit Director; above AED 250,000 requires CFO, CEO and Internal Audit Director. If service receipt, vendor entitlement, amount or legal obligation remains disputed, no payment is released and the matter goes to Legal and the Contract Owner.

### PRC-002-5.8

> Invoice age is measured from the documented service-completion or goods-receipt date to the timestamp when Accounts Payable first receives a valid invoice through an approved submission channel. A valid invoice must identify the supplier legal entity, unique invoice number and date, currency and amount, applicable tax information, and the relevant PO, contract or service reference. That timestamp fixes the lateness band even if internal review continues beyond day 180. If the submission lacks a required minimum field, Accounts Payable must reject it with recorded reasons within two business days and the age clock continues until a corrected valid invoice is received. A later administrative correction does not reset the timestamp; a change to supplier entity, invoice number, taxable amount, currency or underlying supply is material and requires a new validation timestamp and duplicate check.

### PRC-002-5.9

> Vendors and employees may submit an invoice through the company portal where they have access. An invoice may alternatively be sent by email to the Procurement team, which uploads it to the company portal. Email is the permitted fallback channel for vendors with limited portal access. This policy does not provide a portal URL or Procurement email address.

### PRC-002-5.10

> Every submitted invoice must be complete and itemised, include detailed costing, and be accompanied by the approved purchase order and job-completion report. These requirements are additional to the minimum valid-invoice fields in PRC-002-5.8.

### PRC-002-5.11

> The Procurement team completes the Late Invoice Declaration where it is required under PRC-002-5.1. The team uses the template available through its SOP; this policy does not reproduce or link to that template.

### PRC-002-5.12

> A vendor submitting through the company portal receives an automatically generated tracking reference. For an email submission, a member of the Procurement team responds with the tracking number. Submission queries and follow-ups are directed to the Procurement Manager.

## Procedure

1. Submit through the company portal where access is available. Where a vendor has limited portal access, send the invoice by email to the Procurement team so it can upload it.
2. Provide a complete, itemised invoice with detailed costing, the approved purchase order and the job-completion report, in addition to the fields required by PRC-002-5.8.
3. Record the approved-channel received timestamp, validate the minimum invoice fields within two business days, and notify the vendor of any rejection.
4. The Procurement team completes the Late Invoice Declaration when required and uses its SOP template.
5. Use the portal-generated tracking reference or obtain it from the Procurement team after an email submission. Direct follow-ups to the Procurement Manager.
6. If the invoice is missing or irreproducible, request a supplier-issued duplicate first; use a controlled substitute only after the Legal, Tax, evidence, and approval tests in PRC-002-5.6 and PRC-002-5.7 are met.
7. Confirm the service was accepted and has not already been invoiced, paid, or accrued.
8. Obtain Budget Owner confirmation and resolve any purchase-order exception under PRC-004.
9. Accounts Payable validates tax, beneficiary, and duplicate checks, then schedules payment according to approved terms.
10. Tell the vendor the invoice is under validation and provide a tracking reference, not a guaranteed payment date.

## Approval and decision authority

- Budget Owner: confirms receipt and business validity.
- Accounts Payable Manager: a valid invoice first submitted after 90 and up to and including 180 calendar days, even if internal processing later passes day 180.
- Finance Controller: more than 180 calendar days or closed-period impact.
- Procurement: missing or incorrect purchase order or contract.
- Invoice-substitute approvers: the cumulative value bands in PRC-002-5.7, additional to normal payment authority.

## Required records

- Invoice, supplier duplicate, or controlled substitute
- Vendor ID
- Purchase order or contract
- Proof of completion
- Legal and Tax determination where required
- Late Invoice Declaration
- Budget and exception approvals
- Duplicate-payment search
- VAT treatment
- Approved submission channel and received timestamp
- Approved purchase order and job-completion report
- Minimum-field validation or rejection notice
- Original fixed aging band
- Material-amendment timestamp where applicable

## Escalation rule

Escalate missing evidence, disputed service, inactive vendor, bank change, suspected duplicate, or possible fraud.

## Do not

- Do not backdate a receipt or purchase order.
- Do not tell the vendor payment is guaranteed.
- Do not create false delivery evidence.
