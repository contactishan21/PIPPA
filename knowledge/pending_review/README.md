# PIPPA policy candidates — guardrail-safe revision

These three files are **staging candidates only**. They are not loaded by the
application and do not replace the active policy library.

The approved versions of HR-005 (`1.3`), HR-008 (`1.2`) and PRC-002 (`1.11`)
were promoted to `knowledge/active_documents` on 05 September 2026. These
pending-review copies are retained as the review record and are not an
additional source of runtime evidence.

They preserve all currently active clause identifiers and their meanings. The
only procedural wording used is supported by those clauses; unverified claims
about law, systems, channels, payment values, approval roles or external
documents have not been adopted.

Known limitations remain intentionally unresolved:

- `HR-005` confirms the absence-notification channels and company sick-leave
  schedule, but does not name a medical-report upload route, describe any
  unprovided exception to the UAE certificate requirement, or replace the
  controlling legal source.
- `HR-008` confirms the induction structure, roles, access timing and refresher
  availability, but does not provide the titles, web link or itemised content
  of the videos, user guides or SOPs.
- `PRC-002` confirms portal-or-email submission, required supporting documents,
  tracking and follow-up ownership, but does not provide the portal URL,
  Procurement email address or Late Invoice Declaration SOP link.

PIPPA's answer guardrails classify questions needing those details as partial
or unavailable rather than guessing. Review and confirm this staging set before
it is copied into `knowledge/active_documents`.
