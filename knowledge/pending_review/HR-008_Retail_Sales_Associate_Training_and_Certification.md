## Authoritative policy clauses

### HR-008-4.1

> Sales Associates must complete a structured 30-day training track encompassing customer service standards, product knowledge, and loss prevention before operating a register independently.

### HR-008-4.2

> Associates must achieve a minimum 80% passing score on the inventory systems module (including RFID handling) to participate in store stocktakes.

### HR-008-4.3

> The Store Manager or line manager is responsible for planning the cashier training and ensuring that each sales associate receives the required training during induction.

### HR-008-4.4

> An associate who does not achieve 80% may retake the module after documented coaching and remains supervised until certification; the Store Manager records each attempt.

### HR-008-4.5

> A refresher opportunity is available once every six months for employees who are unfamiliar with a process or need to refresh their knowledge. Videos and user guides are available on the company website for self-directed refreshment at any time.

### HR-008-4.6

> An uncertified associate may observe or practise only under direct supervision and may not use another employee's credentials.

### HR-008-4.7

> Sales associate induction includes a structured 30-day training track. Cashier training is part of on-the-job induction and is planned for one full week, during which the employee is buddied with multiple store colleagues for hands-on experience.

### HR-008-4.8

> A buddy is assigned to guide the employee through the applicable systems training, including cashier, RFID and inventory-assessment activities.

### HR-008-4.9

> There is no automated system hard stop based solely on completion of this training. Each employee receives an individual login, which typically takes about two weeks to set up after onboarding. Until that individual login is ready, the employee cannot log in to the cashier, RFID or POS systems.

### HR-008-4.10

> Every Sales Associate must complete both cashier training and sales-associate training. During the first 30 days of induction, Sales Associates must also learn how to receive stock from the warehouse and vendors and how to ship stock from the store.

### HR-008-4.11

> Warehouse Staff do not complete cashier training. They complete their own stock-picking training and warehouse-management modules covering stock receiving and stock shipment.

### HR-008-4.12

> Contractors receive the ethics code of conduct and policy-procedure onboarding relevant to submitting an invoice. This policy does not define the invoice-submission channel or detailed procedure.

## Procedure

1. The Store Manager or line manager plans cashier training as part of the Sales Associate's 30-day induction.
2. Complete one week of on-the-job cashier training with the assigned buddies, gaining hands-on experience under the supervision required by HR-008-4.6.
3. Complete the applicable cashier, RFID and inventory-assessment systems training with the assigned buddy.
4. Sales Associates also learn store stock receiving from the warehouse and vendors and store stock shipment during the first 30 days of induction.
5. Warehouse Staff complete role-specific stock-picking and warehouse-management modules instead of cashier training.
6. Use the available six-month refresher opportunity, videos and user guides when additional familiarisation is needed.
7. Do not attempt to use cashier, RFID or POS systems until the employee's individual login is ready; never use another employee's credentials.

## Escalation rule

Contact the Store Manager or line manager if the required training has not been planned, a buddy has not been assigned, or a role-specific training need is unclear.
